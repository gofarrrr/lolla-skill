## Ninety-second current state

Lolla is an experimental reasoning-pressure skill for consequential AI
conversations. It preserves the available user/assistant exchange, introduces
traceable challenges through four reasoning lanes and a curated mental-model
graph, asks the reasoner to reconsider, and records the process. Its strongest
evidence is mechanical: source and artifact custody, graph-pressure survival,
apply/reject/park disposition, archive, cost, privacy, replay, and inspection.
A separate general conversation-state reader produced unsafe false positives
and is retired rather than integrated. Better decisions, unique real-user
usefulness, market value, and production readiness remain unknown.

| Question | Current answer |
|---|---|
| What is live? | The four-lane pressure skill, repository-local graph snapshot and one-hop planner, reconsideration, disposition, archive, and read-only inspection. |
| What is bounded? | Decision Trail, Product Delta, Decision Work packaging, portable views, and Observatory projections. |
| What is parked? | Mental Model Teacher, broader Atlas product work, and general automatic Decision Work semantic generation. |
| What is retired? | The incremental R4 residual/separated conversation-reader architecture; R3/R4 files remain research evidence only. |
| What is unproven? | Reliable longitudinal understanding, answer improvement, real-user value, market demand, and production readiness. |
| What may start next? | Nothing automatically. Stage 1 Decision Trail truthfulness is eligible but not authorized. |

## Current product map

### Live experimental core

The ordinary supported path is:

```text
preserve available prose
  -> provisional extraction
  -> four kinds of reasoning pressure
  -> constitutional graph pressure survives
  -> reconsider
  -> apply / reject / park
  -> revised answer and memo
  -> archive, receipt, and read-only inspection
```

Active components:

- skill orchestration and run-state guards;
- complete available user/assistant prose custody and declared bounded views;
- provisional `ConversationContext` and `ConversationIR` extraction;
- tendency, mental-model companion, frame, and structural coverage pressure;
- canonical mental-model identity, retrieval, relationship graph, active set,
  one immutable published read boundary, one declared versioned policy wrapper,
  its frozen compatibility serializer, an explicit degraded fallback when no
  snapshot is available, and constitutional graph survival;
- same-context reconsideration and apply/reject/park custody;
- revision, memo, archive, manifests, run health, usage, cost, provider,
  privacy, agent-result, and receipt artifacts.

“Live” means implemented and reachable. “Experimental” means that semantic
reliability and unique real-user value have not been established.

### Bounded supporting systems

- **Observatory:** read-only projection over artifacts its adapters can locate.
- **Decision Trail:** completed-run, checked-in-safe or private-local reports;
  semantically rich fields remain provisional, human-reviewed, private, or
  unavailable.
- **Product Delta:** offline evaluation of completed artifacts; no live-output
  influence.
- **Decision Work:** validation, packaging, triage, rendering, explicit writing,
  and a default-off attachment path for supplied semantic interpretation.
- **Portable views:** bounded Markdown and agent-memory projections.

These systems can exchange artifacts without sharing semantic authority.
Decision Work does not contain a trustworthy arbitrary-run meaning supplier,
and Observatory does not create meaning.

### Parked

- **Mental Model Atlas:** a checked-in, source-bound Phase 1 interface candidate
  exists and carries current V2 repository custody. It is undeployed and parked
  behind founder visual acceptance, native screen-reader review, publication
  rights, and real-user usefulness gates.
- **Mental Model Teacher:** its wider lesson, journey, and learning-product
  program remains parked. Atlas is a bounded exploration surface, not evidence
  that Teacher as a product works.
- **General Decision Work semantic generation:** desired contracts and intake
  exist, but no reliable arbitrary-run semantic supplier does.

### Retired or research-only

- **Incremental R4 residual and separated-surface readers:** retired.
- **R3 and earlier conversation-reader variants:** research only.
- **R4 sources, priors, protected targets, prompts, schemas, requests,
  responses, contracts, runners, manifests, closeouts, and replay:** immutable
  research evidence.
- **Fixtures, fake transports, and test adapters:** fixture/test only.

R4 has no supported path into the live skill, automatic Decision Work supply,
or Observatory meaning generation.

## What is demonstrated

Mechanical evidence is strong for:

- deterministic identity, exact source and artifact custody;
- bounded graph traversal and constitutional pressure survival;
- apply/reject/park ledger completeness;
- request, response, route, usage, cost, privacy, and failure custody;
- first-terminal-result preservation and zero-retry experiment controls;
- completed-run archives, manifests, receipts, replay, and read-only views;
- repository validators and a large automated test suite.

The local Mental Model Atlas additionally demonstrates, within a frozen Phase
1 slice, exact model/relation identity, stable spatial navigation, deterministic
233-record paging, one complete source card on an explicitly partial model page,
one complete relation page,
keyboard/list/table equivalence, reduced motion, and fail-closed projection and
renderer states. The local monochrome study also demonstrates a shared
visitor-facing hierarchy and hue-independent relationship grammar across
Atlas, Library, and the complete Abstraction page. This is local product-
mechanics evidence, not learner-value or publication evidence.

The final R4 A2 experiment also established one narrow semantic result:
splitting two existing residual surfaces into separate provider calls did not
remove the observed opposite-surface companion errors. Both genuine findings
survived, but governed-machinery and companion false positives persisted.

That result supports retiring the incremental R4 architecture. It does not
invalidate all possible future conversation-understanding architectures.

## What is not demonstrated

The repository does not currently establish:

- reliable general longitudinal conversation understanding;
- correct user/assistant influence, adoption, qualification, deferral, option
  lifecycle, changes of mind, stakeholders, or value preservation on arbitrary
  runs;
- that a revised answer is better, safer, or more useful;
- that the four pressure lanes expose unique missed angles for real users;
- independent validation of same-context reconsideration;
- product-market fit, willingness to pay, or production readiness;
- source-rights clearance, native screen-reader acceptance, or real-user
  learning value for the Mental Model Atlas;
- automatic action authority from any receipt, trail, sidecar, queue, resolver,
  or interface.

Test count, clean schemas, polished simulations, provider completion, and
artifact density are mechanical evidence—not substitutes for those claims.

## Conversation and Decision Trail boundary

Lolla keeps the complete user/assistant prose available to the orchestrator as
the authoritative conversational source. It does not automatically capture
system instructions, tool payloads, file contents, or every non-prose context
item. Bounded processing views remain derivative and must record omissions.
For new runs, `agent_result.source_coverage` separates preservation of the
authoritative conversation from coverage of the initial extraction view. A
partial view degrades health as `extraction_processing_view_partial`; the
legacy `capture_truncated` boolean must not be read as source-file truncation.

Decision Trail coverage currently falls into five authority classes:

- **deterministic:** source custody, pressure provenance, dispositions,
  artifact-level changes, privacy, health/missingness, and action prohibition;
- **provisional semantic:** decision shape, current direction, constraints, and
  assumptions/unknowns;
- **human review required:** chronology, influence, adoption, option lifecycle,
  changes of mind, stakeholders, and preserved or lost value;
- **private or locator-only:** raw questions, challenges, and supplied context;
- **unavailable or unsafe:** reliable arbitrary-run unresolved matters, retired
  R4 reopen conditions, and any inferred next action presented as authority.

Missing, partial, failed, complete, and completed-zero remain distinct.

## Constitutional nonclaims

- Graph recall is pressure, not certified relevance.
- Model interpretation is provisional unless human-reviewed.
- A strict schema proves shape, not meaning.
- A receipt proves process, not quality.
- A sidecar or interface does not authorize action.
- The human retains decision authority and consequences.
