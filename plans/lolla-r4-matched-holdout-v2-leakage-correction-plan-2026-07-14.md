## Source-first target rule

For each canonical surface in each case, the protected target must preserve:

- `supported`, `quiet`, `ambiguous`, `partial`, `failed`, and `missing` as
  distinct states where applicable;
- the expected response shape;
- at least three non-adjacent strongest aliases for every supported matter;
- speaker ownership and modal force;
- why the candidate is or is not left outside adopted decision machinery;
- predicted broad-inventory false positives;
- explicit limitations and product-ontology assumptions.

The target must follow the honest source reading. It may not be forced to fit
the intended two-control/two-positive distribution. The runner and every
provider-visible or runner-visible manifest must remain unable to locate or
load the target or its review record.
