# Lolla post-Stage-0-addendum restart roadmap

Date: 2026-07-15

Status: canonical provider-gated restart sequence; no evidence stage authorized

Canonical audit base: `f4493e20634544addd6633d8e92a836c6488f61e`

## Restart rule

Restart from the smallest unresolved product claim, not from the largest body
of existing code. Stages do not authorize one another. R4, R5, task splitting,
prompt tuning, Teacher expansion, Observatory expansion, model comparison,
sidecar automation, and runtime integration remain outside the sequence unless
a later founder decision explicitly admits them.

## Stage 1 — Decision Trail truthfulness on checked-in-safe artifacts

**Product question:** Can a cold human reviewer use the existing completed-run
projection to distinguish source custody, provisional interpretation,
missingness, human-review requirements, and action prohibition?

**Falsifiable outcome:** On a bounded set of existing checked-in-safe fixtures,
reviewers correctly identify those authority classes and material missingness;
any field that looks authoritative without adequate source status falsifies the
current presentation.

**Allowed work:** provider-free coverage packet, human review rubric, exact
source locators, correction of labels or documentation, and register updates.

**Forbidden work:** new semantic reader, private archive inspection, new
fixture, provider call, automatic sidecar supply, R4/R5, or runtime change.

**Provider boundary:** zero calls and `$0.00`.

**Evidence required:** completed-run safe artifacts, field-level authority
judgments, reviewer disagreements, and a non-scalar closeout.

**Stop condition:** any required claim needs private prose, generated semantics,
or action inference; record unavailable and return to the founder.

**Founder gate:** decide whether the bounded Decision Trail is honest enough to
test with explicitly consented real-run material.

## Stage 2 — Consent-bound real-run comprehension usefulness

**Product question:** Does the bounded record help a real user understand a
completed serious conversation later without overstating meaning?

**Falsifiable outcome:** A user can recover the decision path and identify
material omissions; confident but wrong semantic fields, privacy discomfort, or
no incremental value falsify the approach.

**Allowed work:** only after separate authorization—one consented private run,
human review, privacy plan, and corrections to presentation or missingness.

**Forbidden work:** automatic action, broad archive scanning, reader training,
R4 revival, model comparison, or market claims.

**Provider boundary:** provider-free by default; any model call requires a
separate exact authorization and budget.

**Evidence required:** user comprehension, correction burden, trust boundaries,
and privacy observations kept separate from mechanical validity.

**Stop condition:** the record cannot be made truthful without a new semantic
architecture, or the user does not value it.

**Founder gate:** preserve, redesign materially, or defer “understand later.”

## Stage 3 — Live pressure usefulness gate

**Product question:** Does the retained four-lane pressure loop cause useful
reconsideration rather than friction theater?

**Falsifiable outcome:** Under a predeclared human evaluation, pressure exposes
a material missed angle without unacceptable distraction, coercion, or loss of
useful original value. Mere disposition or answer change does not pass.

**Allowed work:** bounded human study design using the current live architecture
and existing custody; no architecture change in the measurement.

**Forbidden work:** optimizing prompts during evaluation, combining usefulness
with Decision Trail semantics, or interpreting graph recall as value.

**Provider boundary:** separately authorized only after study freeze, with exact
call and cost limits.

**Evidence required:** source-first human judgments, pressure-specific value,
harms, preserved value, and user experience—non-scalar.

**Stop condition:** pressure is not uniquely useful, creates friction theater,
or cannot be evaluated without changing the system.

**Founder gate:** keep pressure as product core, redesign materially, or stop.

## Stage 4 — Product architecture decision

**Product question:** Which subset of preserve, pressure, reconsider, and record
has earned product investment?

**Falsifiable outcome:** At least one coherent end-to-end user job has both
truthful interfaces and human usefulness evidence. If none does, defer or close
the current product version.

**Allowed work:** synthesize Stages 1–3, choose one architecture, and create a
separate implementation plan.

**Forbidden work:** silently combining weak components, treating receipts as
quality, or starting implementation before the founder choice.

**Provider boundary:** no provider calls in the decision itself.

**Evidence required:** mechanical, semantic, human-usefulness, privacy, and
market hypotheses kept as separate dimensions.

**Stop condition:** no coherent evidence-backed product job exists.

**Founder gate:** implement one bounded architecture, defer, or close.
