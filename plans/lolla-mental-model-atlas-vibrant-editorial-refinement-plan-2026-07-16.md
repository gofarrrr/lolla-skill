## Falsifiable question

Can the existing source-bound `/models/abstraction` tracer adopt the founder's
dark-blue, teal, lime, and purple palette as a disciplined product system—over a
quiet gray field—without muddy mixed accents, color-only graph meaning, loss of
exact relationship custody, navigation disorientation, mobile overflow, or an
expansion into the global Atlas?

The refinement fails if any of the following is true:

- the supplied bright colors become small low-contrast text or decorative noise;
- ally, tension, and antagonist can be distinguished only by color;
- an exact relationship, authored direction, source boundary, or nonclaim changes;
- a major page-layer link lands under the sticky header;
- a visible mobile control is smaller than 44 by 44 pixels;
- the page creates document-level overflow at 390 or 320 pixels;
- reduced-motion, failed-data, empty/unavailable, loading, disabled, hover,
  pressed, or focus-visible behavior is lost;
- the work is described as a redesign of `/atlas`, the other 221 model pages,
  Teacher journeys, runtime, or Observatory.

## Art direction

The chosen direction is a **vibrant editorial field guide**:

- cool-gray instrument paper provides the quiet field;
- dark vibrant blue owns structure, type, direction, focus, and graph ink;
- vibrant teal owns primary action and practical-use emphasis;
- vibrant lime owns the authoritative-source entry and current selection;
- light vibrant purple owns derived/relational layer transitions;
- Familjen Grotesk provides typographic authority;
- IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono separate human reading from custody labels;
- a four-stop signal path turns the palette into functional page navigation.

The bright accents do not encode truth, importance, confidence, relevance, or
relationship type. Relationship type remains written and shape-redundant:
solid for ally, dotted for tension, and dashed with a cross for antagonist.

## Founder gate

The founder should judge three things in the live local route and the final
screenshots:

1. whether the visual identity now feels like Lolla rather than a generic UI;

generation, renderer unification, Teacher journeys, source redistribution, or
a real-user usefulness claim.
