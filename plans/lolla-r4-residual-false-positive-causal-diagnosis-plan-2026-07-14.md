## Decision standard

A separated-surface experiment may be earned without claiming pairing explains
all five records only when the companion pattern is material, falsifiable, and
isolatable. The diagnosis must state which records a split is not expected to
solve and must require genuinely new hidden cases.

A reconsideration-boundary repair is not earned when the existing residual
contract already states the proposed distinction at role, operation, examples,
evidence, and output-rule levels. Another warning or synonym is not a causal
change.

A source-authority repair is not earned when proposal/adoption confusion
explains only a secondary subtype rather than the primary restraint failure.

The architecture stops if no mechanism can be isolated without bundling or if
the apparent next step is only another warning accumulation.

## Completed diagnosis

The five false positives are heterogeneous:

- three admit governed thresholds or a scheduled decision;
- one duplicates a genuine present gap as a future dependency;
- one turns assistant advice into institutional present state.

Evidence retrieval alone is not the common failure. The model uses late
aliases and recovers both genuine targets, but repeatedly selects evidence that
proves machinery or proposals rather than evidence that proves a remainder.
The current residual contract already states the governed-machinery boundary,
so no narrower wording repair is earned. Assistant-proposal authority leakage
is real but explains only one Arm B record, so no authority repair is earned.

Paired completion cannot explain Cases 01 or 02, and Arm A provides paired
zero counterexamples. It remains a material, unisolated explanation for the
two positive-case companion records: the Case 03 present gap is duplicated as
a future dependency, and the Case 04 genuine future dependency is accompanied
by a false present gap. The Case 03 duplicate also occurs in Arm A.

One future separated-surface task-shape experiment is therefore earned as a
diagnostic of that bounded mechanism. It is not a repair or integration gate.
It must preserve source, prior, ontology, evidence authority, model, route,
reasoning, privacy, runtime, graph, relationships, and canonical roles; it must
use new hidden evidence and include quiet controls so governed-trigger errors
remain independently visible.
