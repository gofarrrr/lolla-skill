## Evidence boundary

Paired generation is not a general explanation for the A1 failures. The two
quiet controls failed without genuine companion findings, and the same paired
shape sometimes returned zero. The narrower unisolated pattern is that both
positive residual-arm cases also received an unsupported record on the
opposite surface.

The future causal intervention, if separately designed, is limited to task
shape:

- paired arm: one call reviews both existing residual surfaces;
- separated arm: one call reviews only the decision-gap surface and one call
  reviews only the reconsideration-dependency surface;
- paired output allowance: at most 1,600 tokens per case;
- separated output allowances: at most 800 plus 800 tokens per case;
- prospective maximum: four paired calls plus eight separated calls, twelve
  calls total.

Only the number of requested surfaces, number of returned reviews, number of
calls, singular grammar, and divided output allowance may differ. Complete
source and prior bytes, context order, residual ontology, evidence authority,
zero and ambiguity semantics, record fields and bounds, canonical role mapping,
model, provider route, privacy, reasoning, structured-output, streaming,
retry/fallback policy, runtime, graph, relationship reader, and compiler
behavior must remain unchanged.

Even if separation later removes companion records, persistent governed-
threshold or scheduled-decision errors still block integration. A successful
task-shape result cannot establish reader safety, model reliability, or real-
user usefulness.
