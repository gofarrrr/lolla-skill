## Goal

Design and validate a bounded probabilistic contract that distinguishes a
genuinely distinct remaining uncertainty from a condition, safeguard, process,
or review criterion already incorporated into the current position.

The job remains with the LLM because it requires interpreting messy language
across the whole conversation. Python may validate declared structure and
evidence, but must not decide meaning through keywords, chronology, or nested
gates.

## Required semantic distinctions

### Unresolved matter

Return a record only when a material question remains unanswered or unowned in
the final conversation state. Do not create one merely because:

- an action must occur before execution;
- the current position contains a contract term, written process, or operating
  condition;
- the matter appeared unresolved earlier but later evidence resolves or
  operationalizes it;
- the source describes risk in general.

### Reopen condition

Return a record only when later evidence or an event would materially reopen a
position that is otherwise current. Do not create one merely because:

- the position already schedules a review;
- a safeguard already says pause, stop, or do not renew;
- a metric or benchmark is already part of the adopted evaluation plan;
- the record paraphrases an operating requirement.

### Relationship

Return a relationship only when it adds inspectable meaning between exact
records. Completing with zero is correct when the only available statement
would paraphrase an endpoint or restate that a current position contains its
own safeguard.

- positive fixtures preserve exact aliases and recover the intended distinct
  matter or reopen condition;
- adopted preconditions, safeguards, written processes, and scheduled reviews
  complete with zero unless a separate source-supported uncertainty remains;
- later evidence can resolve an earlier gap without Python inferring that
  resolution from turn order;
- relationship fixtures use exact admitted IDs and may complete with zero;
- ambiguous evidence is not forced into a supported record;
- no deterministic component judges relevance, semantic role, materiality, or
  relationship meaning;
- no runtime, graph, revised-answer, or receipt behavior changes;
- no provider call, model comparison, retry, fallback, or premium model use;
- no scalar quality score is created.
