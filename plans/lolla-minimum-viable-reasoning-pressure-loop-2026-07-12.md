- evaluate whether the protected pressure creates a specific delta that is
  absent from a control response;
- run the quiet path on a new holdout using the explicit v2.4.2 negative
  qualification representation, not on the already-closed failed quiet case.

## Product claim we are trying to earn

Lolla can take a difficult, ambiguous conversation, preserve what happened and how the reasoning changed, introduce a bounded set of independent mental-model pressures through a hybrid probabilistic/deterministic system, and give a fresh reasoner useful material to reconsider—without claiming that every pressure is relevant or that every revised answer is better.

The minimum viable product is one complete reasoning-pressure loop plus its proof of work. It is not a knowledge base, organization-wide comparison system, teaching interface, production runtime, or universal quality score.

## Current state

### What works

- The canonical substrate contains 222 unique mental-model IDs with a 1,358-edge relationship graph.
- Canonical identity, exact evidence custody, hashes, bounded schemas, fact-leak checks, and no-retry call budgets work reliably.
- Role-first paired extraction separates starting position, current position, and qualification better than independent role readers. V2.4.2 is the current development reference because it can also represent an explicit source-linked absence of unresolved qualification.
- Source-reviewed fact-free mechanisms can drive deterministic graph recall. Removing `missing_reversal_condition` changes the recalled portfolio.
- The graph can emit canonical, provenance-bearing candidate portfolios without receiving raw case facts.
- The system now has explicit constitutions preventing deterministic semantic gating, runtime alias invention, candidate pre-filtering, and probabilistic re-domestication of graph pressure.
- The receipt and cold-reader work demonstrates that complete conversation and processing custody can be made inspectable, although it is not the present bottleneck.

### What does not yet work

- Automatic role-record-to-mechanism interpretation is not invariant under harmless source/provider extraction differences.
- The nine-mechanism vocabulary may be too narrow, overlapping, or unevenly operationalized.
- Downstream usefulness has only conditional and case-specific evidence; it is not an earned product claim.
- A fresh-context reconsideration loop is not the current skill runtime. Current reconsideration happens inside an already influenced context.
- We have not demonstrated both a non-obvious useful pressure and a correct quiet rejection on independent cases under one frozen end-to-end contract.
- No graph/runtime integration is authorized.

### What we learned to stop doing

- Do not pass raw role prose or case facts into graph routing.
- Do not use brittle deterministic gates to understand messy conversation.
- Do not ask another LLM to delete graph candidates before reconsideration.
- Do not treat canonical names as semantic applicability definitions.
- Do not force a candidate to be selected merely to avoid abstention.
- Do not use “better answer” or one scalar score as the evaluation target.
- Do not retry completed cases after a frozen stop rule fires.

## Governing architecture

```text
authoritative conversation
  → probabilistic source-linked role interpretation
  → probabilistic controlled fact-free mechanism hypotheses
  → deterministic canonical graph recall
  → bounded no-deletion pressure portfolio
  → fresh-context probabilistic reconsideration
  → explicit apply / reject / park dispositions
  → revised output plus self-explanatory reasoning receipt
```

Probabilistic components interpret meaning and decide how a pressure affects the case. Deterministic components validate custody, control vocabularies, enforce budgets and bounds, traverse the graph, and preserve provenance. Neither side pretends to do the other's job.

## Phased roadmap

### Phase 1 — Freeze the mechanism-bridge baseline

Build one provider-free multi-case contract from preserved source/provider/ablation records. Reassess the nine mechanisms against the product target and identify overlap, missing coverage, and target errors. Define non-scalar gates before any call.

Exit gate: every case has source-reviewed role records, mechanism targets, protected ablation, and a clear distinction between pattern recall and established truth.

### Phase 2 — Stabilize probabilistic mechanism interpretation

Use small, controlled semantic jobs. Compare exhaustive mechanism review, near-neighbor decisions, or a revised controlled vocabulary without adding deterministic semantic logic. Use new transfer cases after development cases.

Exit gates:

- source/provider protected-mechanism invariance across more than one case;
- meaningful ablation removes the protected mechanism without disturbing unrelated mechanisms;
- no systematic six-label saturation or blanket false abstention;
- exact role-record custody and fact-free output;
- source review confirms that prospective targets themselves are not double-counted or mislabeled.

### Phase 3 — Seal the pressure portfolio

Convert controlled mechanisms into canonical graph recall using declared mappings and graph versions. Preserve every bounded candidate with mechanism provenance and compact challenge material. Do not add a relevance selector.

Exit gates:

- deterministic replay gives identical portfolios for identical mechanism projections;
- source/provider invariant mechanisms yield invariant portfolios;
- protected ablation changes the intended portfolio neighborhood;
- portfolio caps do not silently erase candidates under semantic rules;
- all model IDs resolve canonically and no facts enter routing.

### Phase 4 — Fresh-context reconsideration

Give a fresh reasoner the authoritative conversation, original answer or working position, and bounded pressure portfolio. Require explicit `apply`, `reject`, or `park` disposition for every candidate. Rejection is allowed and preserved.

Exit gates:

- every candidate receives a disposition and exact provenance survives;
- the reasoner does not treat graph recall as proof;
- the revised output preserves strong original reasoning;
- pressure does not create unsupported case facts;
- quiet cases remain quiet rather than accumulating public bloat.

### Phase 5 — Demonstrate product value

Use independent, realistic, multi-turn ambiguous conversations. Pre-review one case where a non-obvious pressure should matter and one where graph candidates should be rejected or parked.

Exit gates:

- at least one pressure creates a specific, source-reviewable improvement in framing, condition, alternative, uncertainty treatment, or reversal logic;
- at least one case correctly stands down without forced change;
- differences are attributable to declared pressure lineage rather than generic second-pass elaboration;
- source reviewers can disagree without collapsing the result into a scalar score.

### Phase 6 — Complete the proof-of-work artifact

Assemble the exact conversation, role interpretations, mechanism hypotheses, graph portfolio, candidate dispositions, original and revised outputs, uncertainties, rejected paths, hashes, models, prompts, and call budget into one self-explanatory artifact.

Exit gates:

- a cold reader reconstructs what happened and why without repository context;
- facts, interpretations, deterministic recall, and later judgments remain visibly separate;
- the artifact does not imply reasoning quality from activity counts;
- only then decide whether skill/runtime integration has been earned.

## What “good” means

Good is not universal improvement. Good means:

- the conversation was heard accurately enough to preserve its reasoning trajectory;
- the deterministic layer introduced traceable pressure that was not silently filtered away;
- the fresh reasoner had to inspect that pressure and could reject it;
- at least sometimes, a non-obvious lens materially changed the reasoning;
- the full process is inspectable and accountable;
- failures and uncertainty remain visible.
