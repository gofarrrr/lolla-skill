# Conversation-state handoff v1 — provider-free replay

The minimal representation can carry the state that the current extraction
lost, but this does not yet mean the model can populate it reliably.

Five source-reviewed packets passed deterministic validation. Together they
contain five jointly developed current positions, four addressed-but-unresolved
threads, one resolved thread, 43 load-bearing constraints, six source-strength
modes, and 88 character-exact source references. Every case-local item is
explicitly ineligible for direct graph routing. The sealed fact-free routing
boundary therefore contains zero graph seeds and zero case context.

This closes a representation question: Lolla does not need a large new
architecture to express proposal ownership and thread disposition. One compact
shadow packet is sufficient. Deterministic validation can check exact quotes,
turns, IDs, allowed vocabularies, and impossible state combinations without
deciding what the conversation meant.

It does not close the extraction question. The packets were source-reviewed in
the same project session. They are not new model outputs, independent gold
labels, or evidence of better automatic extraction. The 53 case-local state
items are also an audit/navigation inventory, not material to dump into the
fresh reasoner's context. A later reasoning-pattern abstraction and compact
pressure handoff remain separate.

The next justified experiment is a bounded extraction-only probe after its
prompt, response schema, two development cases, cost ceiling, zero-retry rule,
and source-first scoring contract are frozen together. No full pipeline, graph,
or downstream answer call is justified by this replay.
