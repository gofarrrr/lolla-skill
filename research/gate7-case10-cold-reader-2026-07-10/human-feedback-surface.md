# Case 10 receipt — compact human feedback surface

This is the shortest honest version of what the fresh-reader test established.

## What we tested

We packaged the complete synthetic conversation, the system's interpretation,
three selected pressures, the Lolla-versus-strong-control experiment, the blind
review, the graph-attribution mistake and repair, costs, authorizations, and
non-claims into one self-contained receipt. A fresh model received only that
receipt and one reconstruction task.

## What the fresh reader understood

- The 925000-dollar ceiling was the user's personal boundary, not validated
  affordability or a correct bid.
- Earlier assistant advice contained invented precision and future stories that
  had to be withdrawn or qualified.
- Three pressure items survived Stage A; the final treatment used two and
  correctly deferred one rather than inventing missing total-cost facts.
- The treatment and strong control produced no material immediate-action
  difference. Lolla's narrower value was explicit, inspectable consideration.
- The graph did reach the reconsideration context indirectly, but this case did
  not isolate a graph-specific contribution and did not authorize graph testing.

## What it missed or overstated

- It omitted the imminent deadline and exact raise-to-925000-then-walk plan from
  the source-state summary.
- It called recorded execution completeness “proven,” confusing strong custody
  evidence with proof.
- It described graph non-use too categorically; only exact graph contribution
  was unidentifiable.
- It asked questions about the house rather than questions about whether this
  receipt is useful to a future collaborator.
- The receipt's pre-call authorization snapshot had become stale after the call.

## Current conclusion

The receipt appears directionally useful for transferring a complicated run to
another agent. It is not yet proven useful to a human, complete, certification-
grade, evidence of a better answer, or evidence that the graph works. No runtime
integration is authorized.
