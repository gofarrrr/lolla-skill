## Decision

Promote the structural custody and extraction-operability repairs. Keep the
joint-process interpreter, fact-free routing, semantic kernel, and expanded
portfolio in research. The live graph/runtime receives no new semantic input.

The next paid step is not another holdout immediately. First run one clean,
prospectively frozen extraction-admission smoke on a designated non-holdout
conversation with:

- output-path preflight;
- direct OpenAI-only embedding policy;
- extraction, matcher, pipeline, and sealer hashes;
- persisted usage metadata;
- exact capture and zero quote-failure gates.

Only after that smoke passes should one remaining untouched case receive a new
paired contract. Future joint-process scoring separates routing surface
(`active`, `edge`, `non-active`) from exact audit-history labels; any allowed
non-active label set is frozen prospectively.
