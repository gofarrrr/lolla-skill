# Batch 4 — integration and simplification decision

Status: **complete**

## Decision

Keep the existing live Lolla core as an experimental product. Keep the newer
semantic kernel, portfolio expansion, and fact-free pattern routing in shadow.
Do not promote them into the live graph yet.

This is not a stop-development decision. It is a decision to spend the next
development cycle on exact accountability and a clearer semantic target
instead of adding architecture.

## Why

The four important reads do not collapse into one verdict:

| Evidence | What it supports | What it blocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Batch 1 | Lolla pressure can add a unique outcome matrix, transition economics, and stakeholder sequence beyond a strong fresh rethink | Both arms retained an unsupported governance claim, so the clean integration gate failed |
| Batch 2 | A strange edge lens can receive a serious private hearing while the public answer correctly stands down | Pressure identity and visible/private effect custody were inconsistent |
| Batch 3 | A simple deterministic sealer can enforce a fact-free projection and reproducible declared routing | The LLM abstraction was not invariant to irrelevant fact changes and the reasoning target was ambiguous |
| Earlier quiet/control work | Strong controls are necessary; unchanged advice can still contain useful calibration or process value | Lolla cannot claim unique answer improvement merely because a revision looks better than the original answer |

The system is therefore useful enough to continue, but not clean enough to
promote the experimental semantic and graph path.

## What works now

- Complete-conversation capture and artifact custody are strong in evaluated
  runs.
- Source validation, hashes, typed contracts, bounded calls, and visible
  failures work.
- The live pressure machinery can surface consequential questions.
- Same-session reconsideration can produce decision-level changes.
- A candidate can be kept as a private guardrail instead of being forced into
  the public answer.
- Direct OpenAI credentials are used for embeddings/query expansion; an empty
  OpenAI key degrades to the non-embedding path rather than borrowing the
  OpenRouter key.

## What does not work well enough

- Conversation interpretation is incomplete and not sufficiently stable in
  role and chronology.
- We have not shown that graph relationships add repeatable value over simpler
  direct criticism or label retrieval.
- The receipt can be structurally complete while misidentifying a selected
  pressure or misreporting its effect.
- The fact-free pattern interpreter does not yet reliably distinguish surface
  substitution from reasoning change.
- The live pipeline still has a large call and attention surface even when its
  monetary cost is modest.

## Simplified product model

```text
PROVEN/RETAINED
full conversation -> source-backed pressure -> reconsideration -> receipt

EXPERIMENTAL/SHADOW
semantic kernel -> joint-process pattern projection -> graph-only routing
                 -> active/edge/weak/parked portfolio expansion
```

The graph remains a curated source of structured difference, not a judge that
selects the right mental model. The receipt remains evidence of process, not a
badge of reasoning quality.

## Next development program

1. Harden pressure custody. Exact packet IDs must survive into every
   disposition and receipt. Deterministic code may enforce identity and shape;
   an LLM or human reviews whether the claimed visible/private effect matches
   the actual prose.
2. Clarify the reasoning target. The routing projection should describe an
   unresolved weakness in the **joint conversation trajectory**, after the
   complete exchange is considered. Actor-specific observations remain useful
   audit evidence but do not automatically become graph seeds.
3. Run one new paired holdout after that hardening: strong fresh control versus
   the same context plus a compact pressure packet, frozen before either call.
4. Retest the clarified pattern target on new invariance pairs. Do not tune or
   reuse the frozen Batch 3 cases.
5. Reconsider minimal promotion only if source fidelity, exact custody,
   accountable consideration, useful incremental value, invariance, and
   operability pass together.

No new reader, agent loop, deterministic semantic gate, graph expansion,
knowledge base, Teacher surface, or SDK is on this critical path.

## The practical goal

The next milestone is not “make Lolla smarter everywhere.” It is:

> Demonstrate one clean run in which Lolla preserves the conversation,
> contributes a pressure a strong fresh reasoner would otherwise miss or
> insufficiently consider, does not force that pressure, and records its exact
> treatment without unsupported claims.

If that milestone remains elusive after the clarified target and new holdout,
the graph's answer-generation role should be reduced and Lolla should lean
more explicitly into conversation preservation, audit, and structured
challenge.
