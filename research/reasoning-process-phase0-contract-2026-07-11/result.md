## Plain conclusion

The technical boundary is now explicit and executable. Lolla's final memo is a
separate object. The reasoning-process path consists of the authoritative
conversation, a broad source-linked ledger, bounded question-specific views,
and an evidence-vector assessment that cannot emit a scalar quality, effort,
or trust score.

The contract makes the hybrid boundary enforceable. Probabilistic readers or
source reviewers own semantic inclusion, parking, relations, and
interpretation. Deterministic code owns identity, exact source validation,
lineage, full input disposition, budgets, quarantine, replay, and forbidden
product boundaries. Facts remain ineligible for direct graph routing.
