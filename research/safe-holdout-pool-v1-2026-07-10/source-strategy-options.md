conversations that:

- involve a serious but non-emergency strategic decision;
- contain several user/assistant exchanges, changing context, and at least one
  correction or resistance to the assistant's frame;
- avoid medical treatment, legal/regulatory action, child safety, self-harm,
  employment retaliation, housing emergency, political persuasion, and major

- do not include expected mental models, graph targets, or a gold answer.

review candidates in that order for safety only. We will not choose the case
