## Decision

Do not spend a third generation call on this case.

The portfolio shape is mechanically valid and preserves more inspectable graph
possibility than the five-item active handoff. But this case's live Step 6
surface was not actually limited to those five items. The archived run already
gave Step 6 a 9,000-character private table with 25 source items and a separate
16-transaction V60 consideration ledger. The five-item handoff was an eval
slice, not the whole live cognitive surface.

A new call here would therefore be a post-hoc test on a saturated case. It
would be more likely to restate pressure that the archived revised answer
already metabolized than to tell us whether edge preservation helps on a hard,
non-obvious case. That is low information value and risks tuning after seeing
the earlier A/B result.

## Mechanical Result

The shadow builder proves only shape and custody:

- all 5 active handoff items survived;
- 7 edge receipts, 3 weak/negative-space receipts, and 4 parked receipts were
  preserved;
- 11 graph references beyond the 3 active graph references remain inspectable;
- all graph references resolve against the 73-candidate graph report;
- the rendered private map is 4,146 of 4,200 characters;
- no model was called;
- semantic relevance, answer quality, and runtime integration remain
  unvalidated.

The 4,146-character result is valid but too close to the ceiling to generalize
quietly. It has only 54 characters of headroom. Future portfolio fixtures
should emit a deterministic `near_limit` warning at 90% utilization while
keeping 4,200 as the hard failure boundary. The response is compression, not
permission to drop edge receipts.

## Eval-Trap Review

- **Premature relevance pruning:** resisted. The portfolio preserved active,
  edge, weak, and parked classes instead of promoting a shortlist as the whole
  answer.
- **Context dumping:** mechanically resisted but close to failure. The map fits
  the cap at 98.7% utilization and should carry a warning.
- **False stand-down:** not demonstrated on this case. The apparently new edge
  material is largely duplicate or guardrail-level after reading the actual
  live table, ledger, and revised answer.
- **Model forcing:** mitigated, not eliminated. Every edge names both risk if
  forced and risk if ignored, and signaling occupies an explicit
  counterpressure slot.
- **Lost ambition / generic prudence:** resisted in the archived answer. It
  preserves a short test, private beta, and legitimate opportunity urgency.
- **Unknown-unknown fabrication:** resisted. Missing prospect authority,
  permission, readiness, and delay mechanism are framed as questions or
  boundaries, not asserted facts.
- **Longer-is-better and smoothness traps:** no credit is awarded for the larger
  portfolio, cleaner shape, or added prose.
- **Answer-delta monoculture:** avoided. A receipt may still have value as an
  accountable rejection, deferral, or private guardrail even when it does not
  alter the public recommendation.

## What This Case Actually Teaches

The useful correction is architectural understanding, not a new answer win:

1. The five-item reasoning handoff is an active eval slice. It must never be
   described or integrated as the complete Step 6 context.
2. The existing live skill already enforces serious private consideration and
   persists dispositions. We should improve the selection and portfolio shape,
   not rebuild that contract.
3. Graph-reference count is not semantic novelty. Eleven additional references
   collapsed to mostly duplicate or partial pressure after comparison with the
   full live surface.
4. The next experiment should search for cases with genuinely unhandled edge
   pressure, not keep adding analysis to a case where the correction is
   obvious.

## Next Gate

Before another paid call, apply the same shadow comparison to the remaining
core corpus and rank cases by:

- active-handoff custody completeness;
- edge novelty relative to the actual private table, ledgers, and revised
  answer;
- whether a novel edge could change an action, gate, sequence, question, stop
  rule, or important private guardrail;
- false-stand-down risk;
- render-budget utilization and public-bloat risk;
- risk that the edge invents facts, motives, precision, or generic caution.

Authorize one exploratory portfolio-arm call only when a case has at least two
trace-supported edge pressures that are not already present in the live Step 6
surface and at least one has a plausible decision consequence. Freeze that
contract before the call. Treat the result as form-factor evidence, not
confirmatory product proof.
