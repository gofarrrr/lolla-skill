# Case 06 receipt v2 — compact human feedback surface

This is the shortest honest account of what the second cold-reader test found.

## What the receipt captured

- The user decided to refuse the 10000-dollar request, offer 2000 dollars, and
  help explore legal-aid and city resources. Affordability, resource
  availability, the friend's response, and the outcome remained unknown.
- Stage A admitted an empathy pressure. The treatment heard its substance but
  returned a different pressure ID.
- The treatment called the empathy pressure a private guardrail even though its
  visible answer emphasized that same lens. The receipt records that
  contradiction instead of calling the run clean.
- Control and treatment reached the same public action. This was a correct
  stand-down, not evidence that Lolla improved the answer.
- Exact V60-affordance lineage was preserved. Exact relationship-graph lineage
  was not present, indirect graph exposure remained unknown, and graph
  contribution was not tested.
- Hashes and call records support custody inside the recorded boundary. They do
  not establish correctness, quality, or better reasoning.

## What the fresh reader understood

It recovered nearly all of that story, including the exact pressure-ID
mismatch, semantic hearing, private-versus-visible effect failure, no unique
public treatment benefit, graph non-claim, partial token scope, and as-of
authorization boundary. That is a stronger transfer than the earlier Case 10
receipt.

## What it missed or blurred

- It did not explicitly say that no deadline was stated.
- It did not surface the user's final conditional inference that rejection of
  the smaller offer would reveal something about who the friend was. That is a
  user belief in the source, not an established fact.
- It said no exact lineage was preserved. The narrower truth is that exact
  V60-affordance lineage was preserved while exact relationship-graph lineage
  was not.
- It preserved operational scope but omitted the exact 38 calls, 6587 partial
  Stage B tokens, and 0.05781025-dollar recorded cost.

## Current conclusion

Receipt v2 repaired several real Case 10 defects. It can transfer the central
run story and its failures to a fresh agent, but the transfer is still partial.
It is not yet human-validated, evidence of answer improvement, graph-value
evidence, or authority for runtime integration.
