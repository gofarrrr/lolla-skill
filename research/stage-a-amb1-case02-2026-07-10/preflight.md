# Ambiguous development Case 02 Stage A preflight

Date: 2026-07-10  
Status: frozen and authorized for one orchestrator invocation

This is a development observation on a same-session synthetic conversation. It
is deliberately not a clean holdout, a product-quality result, or evidence that
Lolla improves an answer.

The selected case follows the rank frozen before the five conversations were
authored. It contains seven user/assistant pairs, late material facts, repeated
user resistance, competing legitimate values, and a provisional ending.

The contract freezes:

- 14/14 message capture and exact-source quote admission;
- the current extraction and full private pressure pipeline;
- OpenRouter Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite for chat calls;
- `reasoning.enabled=false`, verified by one 28-token compatibility smoke;
- direct OpenAI-only embeddings and expansion;
- no revision or downstream answer generation;
- at most 36 OpenRouter calls, eight direct OpenAI calls, and $0.15 estimated
  cost;
- zero experiment retry and one built-in quote-repair allowance;
- source red lines, outer timeouts, exact hashes, and stop rules.

If extraction admission fails, the pipeline stops. If the pipeline or custody
gate fails, there is no rerun. If Stage A passes, the next work is a source-first
review that must distinguish genuinely incremental pressure from repetition,
forcing, unsupported interpretation, and procedural bloat.
