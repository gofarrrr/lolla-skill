# Case 10 Gates 4 and 5 result

Status: **complete; pair stopped after one call per arm**  
Date: 2026-07-10

## Short result

The frozen pair executed cleanly, but Lolla did not produce a materially better
user-facing answer than a fresh strong reconsideration control.

The honest positive result is narrower and important: Lolla made all three
external pressures accountable, used two, and explicitly deferred the
zero-base test because applying it would require invented all-in costs. It did
not force every selected lens into the answer. That is provisional evidence of
**consideration/process value**, not unique answer improvement.

The cost was real. The treatment was about 50% longer by completion tokens,
did not change the immediate action, and introduced an unsupported statement
about household stress. Both arms also failed to sequence evidence tasks around
the tomorrow-noon deadline and omitted some source details.

## What ran

- one complete contract frozen before either call;
- identical complete conversation, system prompt, model, temperature, output
  cap, and typed response schema;
- three source-reviewed pressure questions supplied only to treatment;
- one call per arm, concurrently;
- no retry and no evaluator call;
- blind review sealed and hashed before the arm key was opened.

The run used 6,027 tokens, cost an estimated `$0.021525`, took 9.397 seconds,
and passed every mechanical custody and operability gate.

## Blind result

Before reveal, the review found a near tie and narrowly preferred anonymous
output B for concise user-facing utility. B uniquely retracted the original
assistant's unsupported categorical claim about middle-path bidding. Anonymous
output A was materially stronger as a receipt: it recorded the application,
disposition, visible effect, private guardrail, and forcing/ignoring risk for
every supplied pressure.

The key then revealed:

- A = Lolla pressure treatment;
- B = strong reconsideration control.

The blind judgment was not regraded after reveal.

## What Lolla uniquely gave us

Lolla made the reconsideration inspectable. In particular, it showed that the
zero-base all-in acquisition lens had a plausible application but should be
deferred because verified all-in cost was absent. This is the kind of grounded
rejection that the product constitution says should count as success in the
private reasoning layer.

That did not become a unique action, threshold, sequence, or evidence source.
The strong control independently found most of the same factual corrections
and next actions.

## Why the experiment stops

The contract required the pair to stop on equivalence, unsupported claims, or
process bloat. All three appeared to some degree. Neither arm may be rerun and
the prompts may not be tuned against these outputs.

This does not erase the process signal. It limits the claim:

```text
Lolla provisionally improved accountable consideration and correct stand-down.
It did not demonstrate unique answer improvement in this case.
```

## Gate 6 decision

A paid graph ablation is not yet authorized. A provider-free attribution
preflight is authorized.

That preflight must determine where each admitted pressure actually came from
and define fair transcript-only, labels-only, embedding-only, graph-disabled,
and shuffled-edge comparisons. It must also separate the compact public answer
from the detailed private receipt and preserve hard deadlines before another
consumer experiment is allowed.

Case 10 does not show that the graph was necessary. It shows why graph
necessity is now the right causal question.
