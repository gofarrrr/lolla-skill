  translated into small default-storage, path-check, and supersession rules.

  logic survive in the private-collaborator / product-branch / public-release

  rights, audience, and dependency questions.

Some suppressed labels could produce different wording, but this provisional
review did not find two trace-supported edge pressures absent from the live
surface with a plausible new decision consequence. The paid-call gate is not
met.

## Accountability Defect Found During Triage

The governance run exposed a genuine receipt problem. Its V60 ledger marks
eight readable chunks `not_considered` while explaining that they were
duplicates, already covered, or unsupported. The agent plainly made a
semantic judgment; the status made it look like a custody inability.

The repository contained a contradiction:

- Step 6 doctrine said `not_considered` was only for malformed, inaccessible,
  or technically unusable material;
- the v1 validator allowed it only with `already_covered`, `duplicate`, or
  `irrelevant` routes.

That is now repaired prospectively:

- new skeletons use `v60_skill_consideration_ledger.v2`;
- readable duplicates, already-covered items, and irrelevant items must be
  `rejected` with a strongest plausible application and risk if forced;
- `not_considered` accepts only `route: technical_failure` and requires a
  concrete `technical_blocker`;
- legacy v1 ledgers remain valid and inspectable, so archive history is not
  rewritten or broken.

This is a real product improvement because the receipt can now distinguish
“the reasoner rejected this” from “the reasoner could not inspect this.” It
does not claim that either judgment was wise.

## Next Experiment

The next paid work should create one genuinely informative full live case, not
rerun the two cases already saturated by their own audit surfaces.

Candidate selection should come from the existing corpus and eval evidence.
The best target is a case where:

1. the original answer is not obviously bad;
2. the decision contains interacting or unresolved frames rather than one
   conspicuous error;
3. prior evaluation suggests a material concept can disappear or land in the
   wrong role;
4. a fresh strong reconsideration is a demanding baseline;
5. the full live run can later expose graph survival, private consideration,
   public revision, and custody without changing prompts after output.

Case 07, the messy linked-decisions case, is the current leading candidate
because prior semantic evaluation found an unresolved-decision
self-correction that the semantic packet omitted while the raw transcript
preserved it. Before spending calls, freeze a one-case contract that separates
four questions:

- did the live lanes recover the decisive self-correction from the full
  conversation;
- which graph edge candidates survived or were suppressed;
- did Step 6 seriously disposition them under the v2 ledger;
- did any portfolio pressure add a non-obvious falsifier, question, frame,
  gate, or private guard beyond a fresh strong baseline without bloat.

One full live run is authorized only after its expected artifacts, call ceiling,
stop rule, and no-retuning rule are frozen. No portfolio-arm call is authorized
until that run shows at least two genuinely unhandled edge pressures and one
plausible decision consequence.
