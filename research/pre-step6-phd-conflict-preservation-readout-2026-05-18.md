## Question

Can the existing rendered hybrid surface preserve a genuinely unresolved choice
without choosing both paths or pretending the evidence is already known?

The live conflict:

```text
Silva/data path has higher upside but uncertain access and collaboration depth
advisor-backed fallback is safer but may quietly stop being executable
```

This is different from founder high-clutter. Founder had tension but still
resolved toward a staged partnership. PhD forces Step 6 to keep uncertainty
alive until evidence arrives.

## Result

Pass.

The existing `card_first` surface was enough for this first true-conflict case.
No conflict mode, quiet receipt, bundle, or worker is earned by this result.

The important behavior is that the answer did not synthesize away the tension:

```text
I would not choose the Silva direction yet,
but I also would not default to the safer lab path.
```

That is the shape we wanted. It keeps Step 6 in judgment mode and makes the
decision depend on evidence that can arrive.
