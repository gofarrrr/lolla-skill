## Selection

Case 09, the PhD dissertation-direction conversation, is the next holdout.

It is a better diagnostic than the other untouched candidates because:

- the original advice is materially useful and evolves with the user's
  information, so a fresh control will be demanding;
- it contains both ambition and restraint rather than one obvious bad answer;
- the choice depends on advisor retirement, data access, collaboration,
  funding, field fashion, publication timing, career optionality, and fallback
  design;
- the assistant introduces unsupported numerical and causal authority while
  also building useful evidence gates and an 18-month checkpoint;
- several dependencies may be correlated rather than independent: data access,
  collaborator support, PI approval, advisor runway, and funding;
- a strange lens could help, but sunk-cost, regret, authority, or bandwagon
  language could also distort genuine values or push ambition theater;
- 22 user/assistant turn-pairs provide a real operability test for the new
  12-call Bullshit Index ceiling;
- the case is consequential but does not carry the immediate medical, legal,
  child-safety, or large financial risk of Cases 04, 05, and 10.

## What Makes Case 09 Difficult

The assistant gets several things right:

- option 2 is not viable without infrastructure or a real collaboration;
- option 3 becomes more attractive once specified as known methods applied to
  new data;
- literature, data access, advisor support, collaborator interest, funding,
  committee structure, and a fallback should precede formal commitment;
- advisor retirement creates a real need for another invested faculty
  relationship;
- a precommitted checkpoint is better than open-ended hope;
- the user should not outsource the decision to the strongest stakeholder
  reaction.

It also makes claims that deserve pressure:

- invented faculty-position counts, success probabilities, timelines, and
  career horizons;
- generalizations about retiring advisors, postdocs, reviewers, employers,
  and academic paths;
- a rhetorical swing from balanced exploration to “option 3 checks both
  boxes,” which may overstate how much the dependencies have been validated;
- possible authority borrowing from the advisor's idea and field-fashion
  pressure from single-cell/foundation-model popularity;
- possible ambition theater: treating interesting failure as inherently more
  valuable than a narrower successful dissertation;
- an 18-month go/no-go point that may be too late given retirement, funding,
  and publication dependencies;
- collaborator incentives and authorship/governance treated as inferred rather
  than negotiated terms;
- data access treated as a binary gate even though dataset suitability,
  publication rights, method transfer, ownership, and support may fail
  separately.

## Admission Boundary

The saved historical extraction is not reusable because it contains one
fabricated reasoning passage. A fresh extraction is required and may use only
the extractor's built-in single quote-repair path. If any fabricated passage
survives, the experiment stops before the pressure pipeline.

The pipeline then has separate ceilings:

- extraction: at most 2 calls including the one allowed quote repair;
- core pressure and structural work: at most 22 calls;
- Bullshit Index: at most 12 calls;
- total OpenRouter calls in the pipeline result: at most 36;
- OpenAI embedding/expansion calls: at most 8;
- estimated total pipeline cost: at most $0.15.

A strong fresh control is one additional call, but it is authorized only after
the extraction and pipeline gates pass. A portfolio-consumer call remains
blocked until the table/V60 material contains at least two trace-supported
pressures absent from the control and at least one has a plausible decision or
guardrail consequence.

No candidate count, graph count, model name, or longer answer can satisfy that
gate by itself.
