## What the system actually added

Most selected material was not new. The original assistant already used regret,
margin of safety, calculated risk, renovation staging, and spouse alignment.
Repeating those ideas or naming their mental models earns no credit.

Three narrower pressures survived source-first review:

1. Size the buffer from verified repair, insurance, financing, and household-
   liquidity evidence rather than the assistant's invented `$8–15K`, `$20K`,
   or `$60K` ranges.
2. Keep regret in both directions as a values signal, but do not let asking
   price, current bid, spouse preference, winning/losing language, or vivid
   imagined futures substitute for probabilities and economics.
3. Apply a clean-sheet all-in acquisition test without diagnosing endowment or
   inventing alternative houses.

The unsafe or forcing material was explicitly rejected. In particular, the
Lane 3 suggestion to delay the entire renovation package conflicts with the
user's reported year-one electrical constraint; requiring at least three
options would manufacture process; and an endowment-effect diagnosis is not
supported because the couple does not own the house.

## Important architectural clue

The main delta card produced no findings. The incremental material came from
the companion/V60 affordance layer and the protected frame reserve. That is
directionally consistent with Lolla's thesis that useful pressure may live
outside the obvious active path, but one case cannot prove the graph is needed.
Gate 4 must test whether a fresh strong transcript-only reasoner recovers the
same corrections and whether the supplied packet helps without forcing or
bloating the answer.

## Decision

Gate 4 contract construction is authorized. Gate 4 calls are not. Before either
arm runs, one contract must freeze both prompts, identical model/configuration,
typed output shape, blind labels, the exact three-question pressure packet,
source red lines, and the one-call-per-arm stop rule.

Unknowns remain substantial: a strong control may recover all three pressures;
the treatment may over-absorb them; the clean-sheet lens may be redundant; and
the private reserve may be useful only for process accountability rather than
answer improvement. Gate 4 exists to distinguish those possibilities.
