Relationship label: Inversion and Premortem form an ally pair. ## Practice Reps

### Name The Feeling Without Letting It Rule

1. Name the tempting first read.
2. What feeling is present here, and what rule would still be fair if that feeling changed?
3. Name the boundary that would change confidence.

Answer criteria:
- The answer names the emotional signal without letting it become the whole decision.
- The answer states a fair rule that would still apply if the feeling changed.
- The answer names a transition or failure condition that would change confidence.

Worked example:

- Weak first read: The cofounder situation is emotionally hard, so either empathy or removal should dominate the answer.
- Better question: What feeling is present, what fair rule would still apply if that feeling changed, and what failure would prove the transition unsafe?
- Stronger answer: Treat loyalty, grief, or trust as data; apply a role-clarity and accountability rule; then premortem customer, team, and authority failure conditions.
- Confidence boundary: The lesson would weaken if the role facts, authority facts, or transition risks were still too unresolved to apply the rule.
