## Plain-language result

The next reasoning call is now designed, bounded, and reproducible. No money
was spent to reach this point.

The handoff gives a fresh reasoner the complete Case 01 conversation and nine
active mental-model pressure candidates. It requires one accountable
`apply`, `reject`, or `park` decision for every candidate. The fresh reasoner
must first state the strongest plausible use of a lens, then explain its fit,
the risk of forcing it, and what evidence would reopen or overturn the
decision.

The important constitutional repair is explicit: all three residual questions
reach deterministic direct and graph recall even though the earlier coverage
reader marked them `operationalized`. Coverage remains useful receipt metadata,
but it cannot veto independent pressure.

## What is in the handoff

- the full 24-message, 12-turn authoritative conversation;
- all three source-grounded residual challenge questions;
- six deterministically admitted direct candidates;
- three deterministically admitted graph candidates;
- exact active seed and graph provenance;
- three direct and 23 graph reserve candidates preserved in the persisted
  packet with exact custody;
- a strict response schema that requires complete candidate accounting;
- an explicit boundary that mental models and graph edges are not evidence
  about the case;
- permission for the original conclusion to survive and for the public answer
  to stand down if the pressure is noise.

The full reserve is not copied into the provider prompt as active semantic
work. The prompt receives reserve identities, counts, and the persisted packet
hash. Exact reserve edge provenance remains inspectable in the local artifact.
This reduced the prompt from 54,589 to 30,646 UTF-8 bytes without deleting
custody or asking a probabilistic filter to choose what survives.
