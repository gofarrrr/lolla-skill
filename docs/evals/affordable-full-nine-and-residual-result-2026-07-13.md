## Plain-language result

Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite is useful for small, explicit semantic jobs. It is not yet
proven as a complete Lolla reasoning operator.

The affordable program now shows:

- one correct full-nine stand-down on Case 07;
- source-faithful nine-mechanism interpretation on pressure-expected Case 01;
- a product-level false stand-down because the useful off-frame pressure was
  absent from the role/mechanism representation;
- one verbose residual-discovery failure that partially approached the service
  issue but truncated;
- one seed-only residual run that completed reliably but returned three already
  operationalized questions and missed the protected post-grant and land-use
  opportunities;
- accurate minimal-reasoning coverage judgments for all three returned seeds;
- a provider-free correction that prevents coverage from filtering seeds out
  of deterministic direct and graph recall.

The decisive lesson is not “buy a smarter model.” It is that the product needs
both modes of pressure:

1. a probabilistic interpretation of what happened in the conversation; and
2. an independent deterministic recall layer whose candidates remain
   inspectable even when the conversation-aware model says the nearby concern
   was already covered.

## What works

- Gemini Lite reliably handles small role, qualification, factor, and coverage
  schemas when the semantic job is narrow.
- Minimal reasoning is the best current setting for tiny coverage tasks. In the
  latest seed run all three coverage calls used zero reasoning tokens and
  returned valid JSON.
- Separate discovery and coverage contracts preserve the distinction between
  “a question is plausible” and “the conversation already operationalized it.”
- Exact evidence IDs, packet hashes, candidate caps, complete joins, costs, and
  failures remain deterministic and auditable.
- Case 07 shows restraint: the machinery can avoid adding public pressure when
  the captured concerns were already converted into safeguards.
- The new provider-free graph recall gives every valid residual seed canonical
  direct and graph candidates regardless of its coverage label.

## What does not work yet

- The role plus nine-mechanism lane cannot discover a challenge absent from its
  represented reasoning roles.
- The residual generator did not cleanly recover either protected Case 01
  opportunity after two variants:
  - recurring deployment, storage, shuttle, training, and incident-review
    capacity after capital funding and political attention end;
  - later land-use or service expectations created by protecting one corridor.
- The verbose variant asked the cheap model to author too much and truncated.
- The seed-only variant was structurally reliable but stayed near already

- No fresh reasoner has yet inspected the deterministic direct and graph
  candidates, so useful reconsideration remains untested.
- No park case, real-user conversation, full affordable corpus, graph-value
  comparison, or production stability claim exists.

## Constitutional correction

The first residual design used joint coverage to place operationalized seeds in
a covered receipt and blocked their graph handoff. That was too aggressive. It
risked the constitution's “probabilistic re-domestication” evil: the LLM that
understood the original conversation could remove the independent graph lens
before a fresh reasoner inspected it.

The provider-free correction now does this:

```text
model-authored seed
  -> coverage label retained as receipt metadata
  -> deterministic canonical direct recall for every seed
  -> deterministic graph expansion for every seed
  -> later fresh reasoner must apply, reject, or park candidates
```

On the actual three Case 01 seeds, all marked `operationalized`, deterministic
recall still preserved:

- `omitted_dependency` -> active `bottlenecks`, `systems-thinking`; reserve
  `theory-of-constraints`;
- `stakeholder_or_distribution` -> active `game-theory-payoffs`, `incentives`;
  reserve `power-dynamics`;
- `external_break_condition` -> active `inversion`, `margin-of-safety`; reserve
  `premortem`.

The graph also admitted three deliberately noisy relationship candidates and
preserved 23 more in reserve. Their presence is not proof of relevance. Their
value can only be judged after a fresh consumer attempts a grounded disposition.

## Current model decision

Keep `google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite` as the affordable testing candidate for
small decomposed semantic tasks. Do not call it a production default. Do not
use Gemini 3.5 for routine development. Do not shop models to compensate for
the residual-recall miss.

The present evidence supports task-level routing, not one model for everything:

- Gemini Lite: small extraction, factor, and coverage tasks;
- deterministic Python: custody, identity, caps, declared mapping, graph
  traversal, replay, and cost enforcement;
- fresh reconsideration model: still unselected and untested on the corrected
  portfolio.

## Claims not made

This result is not proof that Lolla improves decisions, that more computation

cheap microtask operator, a concrete residual-recall failure, and a corrected
boundary for the next product-value test.
