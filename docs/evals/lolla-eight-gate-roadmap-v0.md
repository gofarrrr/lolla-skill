Status: active restart-safe roadmap

This is the closest development roadmap. The gates are evidence gates, not
eight equal product features and not a percent-complete score.

## Where we stand

Five gates are complete, Gate 6 is honestly blocked, and Gate 7 now has two
partial fresh-agent transfer observations. This is not a percent-complete
score. It means the trustworthy execution boundary, one full Stage A path, one
properly frozen paired review, and two bounded receipt probes are evidenced.
The repaired Case 06 probe is stronger than Case 10, but Gate 7 is not complete.
Unique answer improvement, graph necessity, human receipt usefulness, and
runtime readiness remain unproven.

A separate designed-ambiguity development lane now contains five realistic
seven-pair conversations. It does not unblock Gate 6 because Codex authored the
fixtures in this same project session. Its first complete full-pipeline
observation is nevertheless useful development evidence: the machinery ran on
14/14 messages for `$0.036504`, but the formal gate failed an understated core
subbudget; companion verification malformed; extraction errors propagated into
at least one false pressure; and the Step-6 table failed to transport selected
V60 mechanisms. The transport defect is repaired prospectively with zero added
model calls. No downstream pair or graph test is authorized from that run.

Gate 3's first attempt produced useful evidence but did not pass. All actual
subprocess, capture, quote, provider, embedding, usage, cost, private-table,
and V60 observations were inside contract. The formal sealer failed because
the runner and sealer used different names for the same extraction-exit fact.
The failed result stays failed.

The field mismatch was then repaired prospectively through one shared schema
and a real runner-to-sealer integration test. The next candidate in the frozen
digest order, Case 10, passed in one attempt: 12/12 capture, six exact quotes,
26 OpenRouter calls, seven direct OpenAI calls, `$0.032496` estimated cost,
healthy pipeline, ready private table, and active V60 custody. Source-first
review admitted three compact pressures and rejected duplicates, unsafe
reframes, forced option counts, and unsupported diagnosis.

Gate 4 then froze the complete Case 10 pair before either downstream call. Both
arms used the same full conversation, model, configuration, cap, and typed
schema. The pair completed in one attempt with two successful calls, 6,027
tokens, `$0.021525` estimated cost, 9.397 seconds wall time, no retry, and no
evaluator call. Every mechanical gate passed.

The blind Gate 5 review was sealed before the key was read. It found a near tie
and narrowly preferred the strong control for concise user-facing utility. The
Lolla treatment did not change the immediate action, but it made all three
pressures accountable and correctly deferred the zero-base test rather than
inventing its missing facts. Its combined public-style revision plus private
receipt used about 50% more completion tokens and introduced a small unsupported
household-outcome claim; public-answer bloat was not isolated. Therefore the
earned claim is provisional consideration/process value and correct stand-down,
not unique answer improvement. The pair is permanently closed.

The provider-free Gate 6 preflight then traced the complete Step 6 consumer
surface. A first reader was preserved as incomplete because it inspected V60
and private-table custody but omitted graph chunks embedded in companion
anchors. The repaired v2 reader found nine raw graph expansions, three graph
relationship chunks in the companion cheat sheet, zero graph-derived V60 or
private-table items, zero individual graph-chunk dispositions, and zero exact
graph-lineage pressures in the frozen Stage B packet. Source-first review
admitted none of the three chunks for a paid Case 10 test. Case 10 therefore
cannot identify graph contribution.

A frozen inventory then checked six comparable July cases. None had the full
chain of passed Stage A, complete graph surface, strong control, frozen exact
graph treatment, source-first admission, and individual graph-chunk custody.
Older scratch runs were excluded because their architecture and review
contracts differ. A provider-free shadow exporter now gives graph chunks exact
hashed IDs and source pointers without changing runtime, but it does not create
semantic dispositions or authorize injection. Paid Gate 6 is blocked pending a
new holdout rather than forced from weak evidence.

Gate 7 then built a 5,345-word self-contained Case 10 receipt and froze a fresh
reader contract before the call. The reader received no prior session context,
expected answers, or scoring rubric. Its single call passed every mechanical
gate with 12,866 tokens, `$0.02457875` estimated cost, 10.899 seconds wall
time, no retry, and no evaluator call. It reconstructed the central source,
three-pressure portfolio, paired near-tie, accountability-only claim, v1 graph
omission, v2 repair, and graph block.

Source-first review nevertheless found material transfer defects. The reader
omitted the imminent deadline and exact raise-then-walk sequence, inflated
recorded custody into proof once, described graph non-use more categorically
than exact lineage permits, and interpreted the human-question field as house
advice rather than product review. The frozen receipt itself invited some of
these failures through softened source-state wording, broad proof language, and
missing as-of and question-type boundaries. These errors remain preserved. The
agent half is therefore a partial pass, not a completed Gate 7.

Those defects are now converted into the prospective Reasoning Run Receipt v2
contract without changing Case 10. Static schema and provider-free cross-field
validation require an explicit source-end action and deadline state, bounded
custody-support language, receipt-freeze authorization scope, separate case,
reader, and human-product questions, four distinct graph evidence levels, and
normalized duplicate rejection. An empty pressure portfolio remains valid.
The schema and synthetic fixture passed Draft 2020-12 validation. The first
attempt to apply it to Case 06 exposed missing exact paired outputs, pressure-
identity custody, effect consistency, partial-token scope, and V60 origin. The
attempt stopped before assembly; the prospective contract was repaired and now
passes 17 adversarial tests. This was contract readiness, not transfer evidence.

The provider-free holdout inventory then found no safe untouched core case.
Case 04 is the only mechanically remaining candidate, but the pre-existing
source-risk review excludes it from a provisional paid experiment because of
legal, regulatory, employment, and retaliation stakes. Every other core case
is completed or permanently stopped. No new Stage A contract or call is
authorized from that pool. Rather than weaken the rule, Gate 7 continued over
the already-closed Case 06 correct-standdown and accountability-failure
evidence. That remained a transfer fixture, not new causal evidence or a rerun.

The Case 06 v2 receipt contains the exact 20-message source, exact anonymous
pair, expected and observed pressure IDs, semantic-hearing and effect-
consistency states, graph evidence ladder, partial operability scope, and an
as-of authorization snapshot. It was assembled provider-free and validated
before the reader contract was frozen. One reader call then passed every
mechanical gate with 10,228 tokens, `$0.0188575` estimated cost, 8.416 seconds
wall time, no retry, and no evaluator call.

The reader recovered the stated decline-10000, offer-2000, and resource-help
plan; the exact pressure-ID mismatch; substantive semantic hearing; the
private-versus-visible effect failure; the correct public stand-down; the lack
of unique treatment benefit; the custody-versus-quality boundary; the graph
non-claim; and the as-of authorization state. It did not inflate custody into
proof. It nevertheless omitted the explicit no-deadline state and the user's
final conditional judgment about the friend, compressed preserved V60 lineage
into “no exact lineage,” and omitted exact operability figures. This is a
stronger partial pass, not complete or human-validated transfer.

The separate attempt to create a new safe fixed holdout pool is now closed
without admission. Five low-risk domains and a mechanical rank were frozen
before any text existed. V1 failed on an unsupported provider reasoning effort.
V2 generated five complete twelve-message cases for `$0.009792`, but its frozen
validator demanded an exact case-prefixed message-ID format that the prompt
never specified. That F8 scorer mismatch remains failed; the conversations were
not selected or repaired. V3 correctly moved canonical ID assignment into
deterministic code, retained identical semantic prompts and ranking, and then
received a preserved provider `429 rate_limit_exceeded` before positive usage.
The terminal rule stopped further model-backed pool calls. There is still no
new holdout and no Stage A authorization.

## Immediate next sequence

1. Treat the ambiguous-corpus reliability checkpoint as complete: provider
   finish errors are truthful and semantically quarantined, the call budget is
   a pipeline-derived custody envelope with separate activation-yield metrics,
   V60 transport is verified against the real Case 04 artifact, and all five
   extraction cases have source-first review.
2. Treat the minimal conversation-state representation and five-case provider-
   free replay as complete. It carries five joint positions, four addressed-
   unresolved threads, one resolved thread, 43 reviewed constraints, 88 exact
   source references, and zero direct graph seeds. This proves representation
   capacity only; the 53 case-local state items remain audit/navigation material,
   not a direct reconsideration context.
3. The extraction-only probe is closed failed. Two provider-schema attempts
   failed before inference; JSON mode exposed first a missing-format-contract
   defect and then the actual Case 03 extraction failure. The final packet failed
   joint-ownership evidence, exact quote grounding, focal-thread recall, source
   strength, constraint coverage, and late-turn trajectory. The stop rule
   prevented Case 04. No graph or full pipeline is authorized.
4. Treat the provider-free conversation-state recovery foundation as complete.
   One typed source now generates local and OpenAI/Gemini projections; all 70
   messages have stable source custody; three shallow microtask contracts feed a
   deterministic candidate ledger and fail-closed compiler; and the frozen
   replay preserves five joint positions, five focal-thread trajectories, late
   Turn 7 contributions, and 45 atomic constraints with zero graph seeds. Four
   adversarial fixtures fail at their intended boundary. This is composition
   evidence, not automatic extraction evidence or provider acceptance.
5. Treat the authorized Case 02 microtask probe as closed informative failure.
   Positions reached inference but every evidence ID lost its `span-` prefix,
   all candidates were quarantined, and the reviewed joint trajectory was
   fragmented again. The thread schema then received HTTP 400 from Google AI
   Studio through OpenRouter before inference; the stop rule prevented the
   constraints call. Two of three calls were used, with zero retries and no
   graph, pipeline, evaluator, or runtime change. A new call requires a new
   contract and authorization. The recommended transfer repair is an
   OpenRouter-Gemini-specific nullable projection plus a source-specific
   span-ID enum and generic focal-trajectory composition, tested on Case 05
   rather than a Case 02 retry. Malformed IDs must not be silently repaired. The
   designed corpus remains development evidence, not a clean
   holdout. Follow
   `docs/conversation-understanding/structured-extraction-practices-july-2026.md`,
   `plans/conversation-state-extraction-recovery-2026-07-11.md` and
   `research/conversation-state-recovery-v1-2026-07-11/`.
   The authorized Case 05 transfer repair is also closed: replacing the
   nullable type array with `anyOf` and adding a source-specific span-ID enum
   still produced HTTP 400 before inference on the first thread call. The stop
   rule prevented constraints and positions. Do not spend more calls guessing
   at strict-schema adapter behavior. The next prospective experiment should
   use JSON object mode, keep the exact typed schema in the prompt, and retain
   all local typed, source, ledger, and quarantine gates. It requires a new
   authorization.
   The delegated end-to-end experiment program is now complete. JSON mode
   reached all three semantic readers reliably, but the one-call-per-family
   design failed Case 05 and both prospectively frozen transfer cases after its
   only generic repair round. Strict constraint recall remained 0.20–0.22;
   source strength, full thread trajectories, and focal current-view boundaries
   remained unreliable. The material-redesign stop fired after 9 calls and
   $0.02172925. Do not integrate or tune this design further. Preserve its typed
   source, local custody, ledger, and quarantine foundation while redesigning
   semantic extraction as probabilistic candidate harvesting plus fresh-context
   synthesis—not deterministic relevance gating.
6. Obtain a genuinely independent safe conversation before any causal paired
   claim or paid graph ablation. Freeze source custody and ranking before review.
7. Resume the compact Case 06 human receipt question when product-transfer work
   returns to the foreground; do not let it interrupt current reasoning-path
   reliability work.
8. Consider Gate 8 only after more than one independent case supports useful
   transfer and a bounded runtime surface. Human approval alone does not waive
   this evidence requirement.
