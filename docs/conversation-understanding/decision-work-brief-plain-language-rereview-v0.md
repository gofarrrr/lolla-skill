## What Improved

The PR123 renderer patch made the top of each brief read like a decision story:

- the decision is named first;
- the action consequence appears before evidence machinery;
- uncertainty has a visible main-body section;
- the "not proven" boundary is easy to find;
- source refs, section uncertainty, custody flags, and non-claims live in
  "Evidence and limits" instead of interrupting every section.

That is a real readability improvement. A busy reader can now understand why
the artifact exists before they inspect the evidence appendix.

## What Still Blocks Use

The strongest remaining blocker is source depth.

The three checked-in-safe briefs still cannot verify:

- raw conversation nuance;
- original/revised overlap;
- user intent;
- lost value or possible overcorrection;
- buyer seriousness;
- compliance tolerance;
- governance, legal, relationship, or patient-risk context.

Cleaner prose makes this more important, not less. A readable brief can create
false confidence if the reader forgets that the source context is compressed
and non-human-validated.
