the grant funds installation while the city still needs recurring people,
storage routes, traffic control, training, shuttle staffing, incident support,
and after-action capacity. The returned record did not identify who owns or
funds that system and cited mostly final safeguards rather than those direct

incident publication. It missed the frozen condition about continued reliance
failing when the recurring operating capability cannot be sustained.

accountability dependency, but it inherited the imprecise unresolved record
and omitted the central grant-funded-installation versus recurring-city-
