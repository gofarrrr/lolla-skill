# Role-record-to-pattern invariance probe result — 2026-07-12

## Decision

The automatic role-record-to-reasoning-pattern bridge is **not validated** and must not be connected to graph routing. The transport and deterministic safety boundary worked; the probabilistic semantic interpretation did not.

This is a useful closed negative result. It identifies the next bottleneck more precisely than another extraction experiment would: the controlled reasoning vocabulary is machine-valid but not yet an adequate interpretation contract.

## What was tested

Two paired cases each had three prospectively frozen arms:

1. source-reviewed starting/current/qualification records;
2. preserved provider-produced records containing harmless extraction noise;
3. a synthetic qualification ablation that retained generic unresolved counterpressure but removed reversal/path-dependence meaning.

One small probabilistic interpreter received only normalized role records. Raw conversation, evidence quotes, expected patterns, model names from the graph, and case facts in the routing projection were excluded. Its response could contain only controlled mechanism, scope, state, and exact source role-record IDs. A deterministic compiler checked schema, exact-ID custody, duplicates, and the fact-free routing projection; it did not judge semantic correctness.

The frozen acceptance rule required source/provider invariance and meaningful ablation sensitivity in both cases. There was no scalar score.

## Operational result

All six strict-schema calls completed and compiled through DeepSeek V4 Flash served by Alibaba through OpenRouter. There were no retries, fallbacks, evaluator calls, embedding calls, graph calls, pipeline calls, or runtime calls.

- provider requests: 6/6;
- estimated cost: `$0.00126427`;
- operational/schema/custody gate: passed 6/6;
- prospective semantic target: passed 0/6;
- source/provider invariance: failed 2/2 cases;
- protected reversal mechanism preserved: failed 2/2 cases;
- ablation sensitivity: failed 2/2 cases.

## What failed

The interpreter routinely filled the six-item cap with loosely plausible mechanisms. It treated the mechanism names as suggestive labels rather than precise contracts. It also chose inconsistent `user` versus `assistant` versus `joint_process` scopes and mostly used `present`, even where the frozen source review represented a joint-process tension.

The protected `missing_reversal_condition` appeared only in the registry provider arm. It disappeared from both source-first arms and the housing provider arm. Source/provider outputs therefore changed under semantically harmless extraction variation. Ablations removed the protected mechanism, but they also changed unrelated mechanisms, so the result was not selective sensitivity.

The key diagnosis is **ontology/prompt under-specification**, not JSON reliability and not yet evidence of insufficient model intelligence:

- mechanism IDs were supplied without operational definitions, positive criteria, exclusions, or near-neighbor distinctions;
- scope and state enums were supplied without decision rules;
- the instruction to include supported mechanisms did not discourage broad label matching strongly enough;
- a six-pattern maximum became an apparent target rather than a safety cap;
- the role prose still contains enough case semantics for plausible over-interpretation, even though facts do not enter routing.

Because the frozen experiment changed several factors between source/provider records, this result cannot quantify how much residual upstream extraction noise contributed. That remains a secondary uncertainty. The systematic scope/state behavior and six-item saturation, however, directly expose the incomplete interpretation contract.

## What worked and should be kept

- the role-record boundary excludes raw conversation and evidence text;
- strict schema and exact role-record custody worked on every call;
- the deterministic compiler stayed mechanical and admitted a structurally valid but semantically wrong fixture, proving it is not a hidden semantic gate;
- `other_review_required` is preserved for review but cannot route;
- the probe was prospectively frozen, bounded, non-scalar, and sensitive in design;
- no failed interpretation reached the graph or runtime.

## Where interest moves now

Do not change the extractor, graph, or model merely to rescue this run. The next provider-free goal is to turn the controlled vocabulary into a real interpretation ontology:

1. define each mechanism in fact-free reasoning terms;
2. define necessary evidence, exclusion conditions, and confusing near neighbors;
3. define `subject_scope` and `state` independently of case topic;
4. decide whether one bounded selection task remains reliable once definitions exist, or whether selection needs small mechanism-family decisions that are merged without chronological or semantic deterministic gating;
5. replay the six preserved candidate payloads locally against the revised compiler and adversarial fixtures before authorizing any new call.

Only a new prospectively frozen transfer probe may authorize more provider calls. Passing that future probe would validate the abstraction bridge for research shadow use only; graph integration would still require a separate decision.
