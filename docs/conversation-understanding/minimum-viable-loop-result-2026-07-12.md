# Minimum viable Lolla loop result

Status: minimum loop proved with preserved limitations; no runtime authorization  
Date: 2026-07-12

## What is now demonstrated

Two new independent, realistic multi-turn conversations completed the bounded
product proof.

In the retailer case, source-linked role extraction preserved the starting
attraction and capacity concern, the current bounded-pilot position, and the
unresolved independent-demand qualification. The protected
`status_signal_used_as_evidence` mechanism survived source/provider
role-record variation and disappeared when that status meaning was removed.
The operational provider projection deterministically recalled eight canonical
candidates without deletion or semantic prefiltering. A fresh reasoner
dispositioned all eight. Compared with its transcript-only control, the
pressure answer added an outside-channel demand test and a generalization check
on retailer context. This is source-reviewed non-obvious useful pressure.

The retailer answer is not a clean reference. It also invented unsupported
numerical examples. That failure is preserved, the completed case was not
retried, and future fresh-reasoner packets now have a versioned no-false-
precision instruction.

In the library case, the quiet-capable role contract preserved starting and
current positions and explicitly authored a source-linked
`no_unresolved_qualification_observed` result without manufacturing a
qualification record. The current plan's modal force was understated, which is
preserved as a fidelity caveat. The mechanism interpreter still reviewed all
nine controlled mechanisms, returned all nine `not_observed`, and emitted an
empty fact-free routing projection. Deterministic recall sealed a zero-candidate
stand-down. No graph traversal, fresh pressure call, or public-answer bloat was
manufactured.

The combined receipt contains both complete conversations, role records,
mechanism assessments, deterministic portfolios, candidate dispositions,
control and pressure outputs, source reviews, failures, exact artifact hashes,
models, calls, tokens, costs, and claim boundaries. A fresh cold reader
reconstructed both paths and every material success and failure from the
receipt alone.

## Preserved cold-reader failure

The cold-reader run's generic typed-output gate failed because its validator
silently required object values to be strings while the receipt and reader used
arrays for allowed and blocked authorizations. The substantive reconstruction
was complete. This is preserved as an evaluation-contract mismatch, not
retroactively changed into a mechanical pass and not retried.
