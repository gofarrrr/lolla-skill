# Reasoning-process graph-impact shadow result — 2026-07-12

## Decision

The paired extractor's observed residual noise does not change graph pressure after a source-faithful fact-free abstraction on the two preserved paired cases. This is useful conditional evidence, not integration authority. The unproven bridge is now automatic role-record-to-reasoning-pattern abstraction.

## Safe boundary discovered

Role records cannot go directly to graph routing. Deterministic pressure routing expects controlled tendencies or mechanisms, and the activation matcher accepts only typed reasoning-shape inputs. Raw role prose contains case facts and must not be embedded.

The existing research-only `ReasoningPatternPacket` provides the safe middle boundary. It permits only controlled mechanism, subject-scope, and state values in its routing projection and lints out quotes, entities, quantities, desired outcomes, and topic labels.

## Deterministic shadow

Codex-assisted source review produced provisional fact-free projections for the registry and housing cases. Each source-first and provider arm retained:

- acknowledged constraint not gated;
- counterpressure acknowledged but not integrated;
- missing reversal condition.

The sealed source-first and provider projections were identical in both cases. They produced the same eight deterministic seed models and identical one-hop supporting/risk neighborhoods from the real `data/relationship_graph.json`.

A missing-reversal ablation removed `commitment-bias`, `premortem`, and `sunk-cost-fallacy` seeds. The projection and seed candidates changed in both cases, proving the shadow is sensitive to a material protected mechanism rather than trivially invariant.

The harness also exposed a pre-existing path discrepancy: `RelationGraph.load(repo_root)` looks for `build/relationship_graph.json`, while this checkout carries the graph at `data/relationship_graph.json`. The shadow now loads the declared data artifact explicitly and fails closed on an empty graph. Runtime behavior was not changed.

## Embedding shadow

Current OpenAI documentation confirms `text-embedding-3-large` at 3,072 default dimensions, matching the stored edge vectors. A shadow-only typed adapter converted controlled pattern nodes into `FingerprintPayload` with empty evidence quotes. No role prose or facts entered the embedding.

One frozen batch request embedded the full and missing-reversal projections: 65 tokens total. The source-first and provider arms reused the same full vector because their sealed projections were identical.

No activation tiebreaker fired. Most candidate pairs were outside the narrow affinity window; eligible pairs were below the similarity floor or lacked stored edge vectors. Embedding therefore did not change full deterministic selection. The ablation changed supporting and risk unions because its deterministic seed set was smaller, not because embeddings reordered a near tie.

## Product meaning

Conditional on a faithful fact-free abstraction, the paired extractor's extra evidence aliases, modal-label errors, coarse categories, inaccurate allocation note, and truncated role prose did not change the controlled mechanism set or mental-model graph neighborhood in these two cases. Protected reversal pressure did reach the graph and materially changed the candidate portfolio.

However, the fact-free projections were source-reviewed. We have not proven that an automatic probabilistic interpreter will map source-first and provider records to the same mechanisms. That is now the highest-risk bridge and the next experiment.

## Follow-on experiment result

The automatic role-record-to-pattern probe is now complete and failed its semantic gates. All six strict-schema calls compiled, but source/provider projections were not invariant in either case, the protected reversal mechanism was not preserved, and the ablations changed unrelated mechanisms. The safe fact-free and exact-ID boundary worked; the controlled vocabulary lacked operational definitions and produced broad label matching. Do not integrate it. The next work is provider-free reasoning-mechanism ontology design, not another graph or extraction call. See `role-record-pattern-invariance-probe-result-2026-07-12.md`.
