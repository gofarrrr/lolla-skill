## Executive answer

Decision: `r4_separated_surface_experiment_earned`.

The five residual-arm false positives do not have one established common
cause. Three convert adopted machinery into reconsideration dependencies, one
duplicates a real present gap onto the future surface, and one turns assistant
advice into institutional present state. The current prompt already says to
subtract thresholds, fallbacks, scheduled decisions, and governed pending
work, so another warning or synonym is not an earned repair. Proposal-versus-
adoption confusion is real but explains only one of the five Arm B records.

Paired completion pressure is also not a general explanation: Cases 01 and 02
failed without a genuine companion surface, Arm A was exactly quiet on both
Case 02 surfaces under the same paired shape, and Arm A found the genuine Case
04 dependency while keeping the opposite present surface quiet. But one
narrower pattern remains material and falsifiable. In both Arm B positive
cases, a genuine record was accompanied by a false record on the opposite
surface; the Case 03 duplicate also occurred in Arm A. Separating only surface
generation can test that companion-record mechanism while retaining quiet
controls to show which governed-trigger errors survive independently.

This earns a task-shape experiment, not a split implementation, integration,
or another provider call. Even a successful future split would not establish
that the reader is safe.

The disconfirming-first assessment rejects the broad causal story, preserves
the narrow falsifiable rival, and keeps integration blocked.

## Evidence against paired completion pressure

Lead evidence against the hypothesis:

1. Case 01 produces two governed dependencies while its other residual surface
   is correctly zero. There is no legitimate companion to “complete.”
2. Case 02 produces a scheduled-decision dependency while its other residual
   surface is zero. Again, there is no legitimate companion.
3. Arm A uses the same paired shape and seed in Case 02 and returns exact zero
   on both surfaces.
4. Arm A finds the genuine Case 04 dependency while keeping the opposite
   present-gap surface zero.

Therefore generic pairing is neither necessary nor sufficient for the quiet-
control errors.

What remains plausible is narrower: a legitimate finding may increase the
chance that the model supplies a companion on the opposite surface. Arm B does
this in both positive cases, and Arm A does it in Case 03. That is correlation,
not causal identification. It is nevertheless material, falsifiable, and
isolatable through one task-shape comparison.

## Product interpretation

The residual reader is better at finding affirmative material than proving that
a tempting candidate should remain absent. It recovered both genuine targets
because the source contains explicit ownership refusals, funding limits, or an
external authority failure. Restraint requires a negative comparison: retrieve
the candidate, seek the owner/process/adoption evidence that defeats residual
status, preserve speaker authority, and then accept zero. The outputs often
retrieve that machinery and even describe it correctly, but still place it on
a residual surface.

This is not simple long-context loss. It is evidence adjudication under a job
that simultaneously asks for two semantically adjacent products. The observed
defect is heterogeneous: semantic-boundary execution is the repeated control
failure; cross-surface duplication is the positive-case companion pattern;
source authority is a narrower Case 04 subtype; and evidence precision remains
weak even on the correct Case 04 finding.

## Final decision

The exact earned intervention is one future comparison between:

- the unchanged paired residual task; and
- the same residual ontology with the two existing surfaces generated in
  separate provider tasks.

Only task shape may change. Complete source and prior bytes, source → prior →
task order, residual definitions, exact-evidence rule, zero and ambiguity,
model, route, reasoning, strict JSON, privacy, fallback policy, runtime, graph,
relationship behavior, and canonical downstream roles must remain unchanged.

The future experiment must include genuinely new hidden quiet controls and
genuine positive cases. A1 is exposed and may define predicted failure classes
but cannot be reused as hidden validation evidence.

Support would require the separated tasks to suppress opposite-surface
companions without losing the two genuine residual types, while the matched
paired arm repeats those companions. The quiet controls must remain visible so
governed-trigger errors are not credited to the split. Persistence of the same
companions under separation falsifies paired pressure. Persistence of Case
01/02 errors would show, as expected, that task shape does not solve the
semantic-boundary defect.

This diagnosis does not prepare that experiment and does not authorize a call.
