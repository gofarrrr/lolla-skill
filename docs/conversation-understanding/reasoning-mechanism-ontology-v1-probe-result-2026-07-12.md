# Reasoning mechanism ontology v1 probe result — 2026-07-12

## Decision

Ontology v1 is a material improvement, but the automatic bridge remains blocked from graph and runtime integration. The frozen six-arm probe passed transport, complete-review, custody, protected-mechanism, and basic ablation direction. It failed exact prospective targets, source/provider invariance, and selective ablation.

## What became clearer

The earlier interpreter's broad six-label saturation disappeared. Every call reviewed all nine mechanisms exactly once, and only one to three were declared unresolved. Both source and provider arms in both cases consistently retained `counterpressure_acknowledged_not_integrated` and `missing_reversal_condition`. Both synthetic ablations removed `missing_reversal_condition`.

This is the first automatic evidence that the protected reasoning meaning can survive both cases and provider extraction noise under a defined ontology. It is not enough for integration.

## Source-first review

The probe also exposed problems in the earlier human target.

`acknowledged_constraint_not_gated` is not clearly supported. Both current records contain multiple operative conditions and thresholds. The remaining irreversibility concern is better represented by `counterpressure_acknowledged_not_integrated` plus `missing_reversal_condition`; adding the constraint-gate mechanism would double-count overlapping pressure without evidence that the acknowledged constraint can proceed wholly ungated.

`upside_downside_evidence_asymmetry`, selected only in the richer source-first records, is unsupported. The records show desired upside, extensive safeguards, and remaining counterpressure, but not unequal evidence standards or weighting. This is a model false positive triggered by richer prose, and it explains most mechanism-level source/provider variance.

The provider arms classified `missing_reversal_condition` as `present`, while source-first used `missing_protection` in registry and `present` in housing. The mechanism identity was stable, but the legacy state distinction remains unreliable and is not needed by the downstream seed mapping, which currently ignores state. We should not hide that discrepancy by deterministic normalization.

## Architectural conclusion

Operational definitions fixed the largest failure mode, but a single exhaustive interpreter still confuses a richly described balance of upside/downside with asymmetric evidence treatment. The next interest is narrow **near-neighbor adjudication**, not a larger prompt, a new model, or more deterministic gates.

Provider-free next work should test a small decision contract that receives the first-pass nine-row assessment and only adjudicates mutually confusable mechanism families, especially:

- acknowledged constraint not gated vs counterpressure acknowledged not integrated;
- reversible path not considered vs missing reversal condition;
- counterpressure not integrated vs upside/downside evidence asymmetry.

The adjudicator must be probabilistic, source-linked, allowed to reject both labels, and merged mechanically by exact mechanism and role-record IDs. It must not become chronological gating or keyword classification. A new call requires a fresh frozen contract and transfer case; no retry of this probe is authorized.
