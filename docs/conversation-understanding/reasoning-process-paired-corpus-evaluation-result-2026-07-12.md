# Paired role corpus evaluation result — 2026-07-12

## Decision

Select a read-only shadow graph-impact study as the next experiment. Do not spend another transfer call yet and do not authorize production, live graph routing, reconsideration, or runtime integration.

## Corpus

The review uses four synthetic cases whose targets were written before their provider calls:

- v2.2 farm cooperative, independent current and qualification readers;
- v2.3 museum AI license, clarified independent readers;
- v2.4 patient registry, paired reader with the now-closed redundant status wire;
- v2.4.1 housing retrofit, status-free paired reader.

The cases are directional development evidence, not independent human gold. Architecture and domain change together, so the comparison is not causal proof.

## Evaluation method

Every preserved output was compared to its source-first target across seven separate dimensions:

1. central current-versus-qualification allocation;
2. protected qualification survival;
3. speaker ownership;
4. evidence precision;
5. expression force;
6. object/category precision;
7. relationship preservation.

Allowed dispositions are categorical: pass, partial, fail, not observed, or operationally blocked. No score, weighting, average, ranking, or automatic semantic judge is permitted.

## Findings

Both independent-reader cases failed central allocation: a merely unresolved meaning leaked into current as well as qualification. Both paired-reader cases passed central allocation, including distinct meanings inside one shared alias.

Protected qualification and material speaker ownership survived in all four cases. This corpus no longer points to basic qualification omission as the leading problem.

Evidence precision, expression force, and object/category precision remain uneven across every architecture. Pairing solved the role-allocation problem it was designed to solve; it did not make all semantic labels exact.

Relationship preservation was partial in both independent cases. V2.4 was operationally blocked before relation by the redundant status contradiction. V2.4.1 produced the first paired relationship pass.

## Why another transfer call is not next

A third paired transfer case would mainly test whether central allocation passes again. It would not tell us whether extra evidence aliases, modal-label errors, or coarse object categories materially change Lolla's actual pressure selection. That downstream consequence is now the higher-value unknown.

## Next experiment

Build a provider-free, read-only shadow that compares two inputs for the preserved v2.4 and v2.4.1 cases:

- source-first target role records;
- preserved model-produced role records.

First compare the graph-input packets themselves. Then, under a separately frozen contract, compare deterministic candidate selection and any embedding-dependent selection without sending reconsideration, changing runtime, or writing product receipts. Preserve exact chunk/model attribution and disagreement. Do not call a model evaluator and do not declare one selection “better” automatically.

The key question is not whether labels differ. It is whether those differences change which mental-model pressure reaches the future thinker, why it changes, and whether protected qualifications remain represented.
