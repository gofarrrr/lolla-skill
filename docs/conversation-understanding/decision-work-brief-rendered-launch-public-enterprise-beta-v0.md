## What changed

**Starting point:**
Likely starting point: The likely starting direction already leaned away from a public launch and toward a private enterprise proof program, with the 900-person prospect prioritized if it accepted strict gates.
Uncertainty: high.

**What Lolla pressed on:**
Pressure point: Lolla appears to have pressed on whether enterprise-logo size, public-launch optics, and generic readiness gates were being treated as evidence before buyer behavior, audit-log limits, support load, and stop rules were specified.
What was pressed:
- whether the 900-person prospect deserved priority by default
- whether public launch answered a concrete investor or recruiting question
- whether gates had owners, thresholds, and stop actions
- whether brittle audit logs, support load, churn, and seven-month runway should constrain the launch shape
Uncertainty: medium.

**Change in direction:**
What changed: The plan changed from favoring the larger logo under strict gates toward making both prospects compete for priority through the same paid, scoped, private pilot shape.
Specific changes:
- The 900-person company no longer gets the first slot by default.
- Both prospects receive the same written pilot shape: paid commitment, one workflow, no public launch, capped support, explicit audit-log boundaries, conversion trigger, and reference terms.
- Priority is based on payment, procurement clarity, tolerance for scope, and usefulness as proof.
- The public-launch thesis must name the investor or recruiting outcome it actually changes.
- Readiness gates become tripwires with owner, threshold, and stop action.
Uncertainty: medium.

## What this means for action

Before the exec meeting or buyer call, the team would write the same private-pilot offer for both prospects, define stop rules for reliability, audit exports, support load, commercial commitment, and customer response times, and choose the buyer whose behavior creates usable proof.
Possible next actions:
- Do not award priority to the 900-person prospect just because of logo size.
- Ask the board observer, investor, and head of sales what concrete outcome a public page changes.
- Convert likely failure modes into contract clauses, support caps, and launch limits before the buyer call.
- Keep public launch as a later marketing experiment only if private proof and named external outcomes exist.
Uncertainty: medium.
