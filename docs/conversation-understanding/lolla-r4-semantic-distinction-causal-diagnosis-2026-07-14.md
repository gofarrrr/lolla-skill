## Contradicting evidence first

The founder's proposed explanation is not established by a clean ablation.
Several facts constrain how strongly it may be stated:

1. **The v2 prompt already contains the intended negative rules.** It says
   that an adopted process, safeguard, benchmark, fallback, or scheduled
   review is not automatically an unresolved matter or reopen condition. It
   also says to complete with zero when no distinct matter remains. The defect
   is therefore not simply a missing instruction or missing zero outcome.
2. **Case 04 contains a genuinely pending technical choice in ordinary
   language.** The source does not select one shared design or two independent
   designs, and it does not enumerate the future redesign milestones. A broad
   reader can therefore call the design "unresolved" without behaving
   randomly. The product target applies a narrower rule: a deliberately
   deferred choice with an assigned mechanism, competition, evidence criteria,
   and fixed decision point is operationalized rather than a newly distinct
   gap. That specialized product ontology, not ordinary English alone, is doing
   decisive work.
3. **Prior anchoring cannot explain every false positive.** Case 04's prior
   qualification explicitly says the long-term technical structure is
   undecided, which points toward the returned milestone record. But the first
   Case 04 reopen record comes from predefined thresholds not asserted as a
   gap by the prior, and the Case 01 false reopen comes from a hydraulic
   precondition and partial-deployment procedure rather than the prior
   interpretation.
4. **Paired completion pressure is visible but not isolated.** Across the four
   completed provider uncertainty tasks in the corrected R4 and semantic-
   distinction runs, every unresolved and reopen surface was non-zero. All four
   relationship tasks were also non-zero. The observed uncertainty record
   counts were `3, 3, 2, 3`; relationship counts were `2, 2, 1, 2`. However,
   the schema permits empty arrays, the prompt explicitly licenses zero, and no
   same-case paired-versus-separated counterfactual exists.
5. **The model did not simply lose the endpoint in long context.** In Case 04
   it cited late endpoint aliases `e094`, `e096`, and `e098`, described the
   technical transition coherently, and used exact current and qualification
   IDs. The semantic error is a reinterpretation of retrieved endpoint
   material, not evidence that the endpoint was absent from working context.
6. **There is no causal identification from two holdout cases.** The run
   changed no factor within a case, and the source, prior, paired task, model,
   and context length co-varied as one frozen package. "Primary" below means
   best-supported comparative diagnosis from preserved evidence, not a proven
   experimental effect size.

These contradictions rule out a flattering account in which one extra warning
would obviously fix the reader. They do not rule out one narrowly scoped
semantic-task repair as the next provider-free unit of work.

## Authoritative semantic boundary used for this diagnosis

The frozen source-first target is the product authority for this consumed
holdout. It makes the intended distinction:

- **Broad inventory:** anything still pending, conditional, reviewable, or
  capable of causing reconsideration.
- **Product residual:** only a materially distinct gap that is still
  unanswered, unowned, or unoperationalized after the current position's
  adopted mechanisms are taken into account.

For Case 04, funding the interface/specification, requesting parallel
implementations, preserving competition, tracking support, testing recovery,
and assigning a criteria-based twelve-month decision operationalize the open
design choice for the current decision horizon. The future design is not yet
selected, but "not yet selected" is not by itself a new R4 uncertainty record.

For Case 01, the final position operationalizes exercises, procedures,
incident publication, shuttle arrangements, and responsibility boundaries,
but it does not assign recurring funding and ownership for the complete city
operating capability. That residual remains admissible. Case 01 is used only
to ensure that stronger restraint does not erase this materially distinct gap.

## Competing causal explanations

### 1. Semantic ontology/task mismatch — best-supported primary explanation

**What would have to be true:** The reader must be treating "unresolved" and
"reopen" as broad inventory categories, while the product uses them as
residual categories that exist only after subtracting what the current
position has assigned and operationalized.

**Supporting evidence:**

- Every Case 04 false positive is natural under the broad reading: the design
  remains unselected, missed thresholds cause exit, redesign slippage causes
  reconsideration, and those conditions relate to the current plan.
- Every one is wrong under the frozen residual reading because the source
  already assigns the decision work to mechanisms, criteria, fallbacks, and a
  fixed review.
- Case 01 shows the inverse distinction. The genuine residual is recurring
  ownership/funding not assigned by the final mechanisms, while the false
  hydraulic reopen is an adopted condition/process. The reader noticed future
  execution generally but did not reliably perform the residual subtraction.
- The prompt's top-level role and task still ask the model to inventory two
  broadly named surfaces. The specialized residual rule appears as a
  qualification inside a larger semantic contract. The output tracks the
  broad surface prototypes more consistently than the residual test.

**Evidence against:** The semantic contract already states the residual test
and gives negative examples. Therefore this is not an instruction-absence
diagnosis. It is a mismatch between the primary job identity and the product's
narrower object, or model noncompliance with that nested distinction.

**Comparative status:** Best fit across all eight records. It explains both
Case 04 overgeneration and Case 01's partial sensitivity without requiring
endpoint loss. It is the only explanation that covers false positives with and
without a matching prior anchor.

### 2. Fallible-prior anchoring — supported amplifier, not primary cause

**What would have to be true:** Even though the source is labeled
authoritative, the immediately supplied prior summaries must shape candidate
generation before the model applies the residual test.

**Supporting evidence:**

- Case 04's prior qualification literally calls the transition unresolved and
  the long-term structure undecided; the returned unresolved record narrows
  that frame to undefined milestones.
- The first Case 04 relationship directly uses that qualification as an
  endpoint.
- Case 01's prior current limitation says viability hinges on city resources;
  the one useful record repeats that operating-dependency frame while missing
  stronger funding/ownership evidence.

**Evidence against:** Case 04's predefined-threshold record and Case 01's
hydraulic/process reopen record lack corresponding prior claims. In both
places the prior current record actually describes safeguards or
operationalization that should reduce admission. Prior context changes salience
but does not supply the shared broad-inventory error.

**Comparative status:** Causal amplification is plausible and record-level
alignment is strong, but a context-authority repair is not independently
earned. Removing or reformatting the prior would not address the threshold and
hydraulic false positives demonstrated here.

### 3. Paired generative completion pressure — plausible, not identified

**What would have to be true:** Requiring exactly two named reviews and then a
relationship review must create a completion prior: fill each semantic slot,
then connect the resulting endpoints, even though record arrays may be empty.

**Supporting evidence:**

- All four provider uncertainty calls across the exposed corrected-R4 and v2
  runs returned non-zero records for both surfaces.
- All four downstream relationship calls returned at least one relationship;
  three used the two-record maximum.
- Case 04's global limitation says records "focus on the decision gates,"
  which reads like an inventory plan for filling both requested categories.
- Relationship false positives appear only after non-zero endpoint generation,
  consistent with downstream elaboration pressure.

**Evidence against:** The wire schema permits zero records, the prompt repeats
that zero is correct, and the deterministic compiler preserves
`completed_zero`. No same-case split task exists, so saturation could instead
come from broad surface semantics, prior anchors, model behavior, or case
selection.

**Comparative status:** Worth preserving as a falsifiable rival, but a task-
shape repair is not earned from this evidence alone. Changing pairing now
would bundle a second causal variable with the better-supported semantic one.

### 4. Model/context limitation — possible background constraint, weak primary explanation

**What would have to be true:** Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite with minimal reasoning
must be unable to integrate the 12,680-token Case 04 packet's multiple source
needles and authority hierarchy reliably.

**Supporting evidence:**

- Case 04 required multi-needle comparison across a long, aliased source plus
  prior interpretations and a strict schema.
- The run reports zero reasoning tokens under a minimal-reasoning request.
- A two-case result cannot establish that this operator can apply a subtle
  residual ontology reliably.

**Evidence against:** The response uses late source aliases, correctly
describes the component transition, identifies the fixed twelve-month review,
and uses exact current and qualification endpoints. It is coherent and
source-local. The observed error is choosing the broad semantic category for
retrieved facts, not failing to retrieve or connect those facts.

**Comparative status:** Not supported as the primary cause. A model or context
change is also outside the authorized scope and would not isolate the semantic
question.

## Frame challenge: is a founder ontology decision still missing?

There is a real linguistic tension: an unselected future design is "unresolved"
in normal usage. But the frozen target, the completed semantic-distinction
plan, and the user's present falsifiable question already select the narrower
product ontology: deliberate deferral with owned decision work does not count
unless a materially distinct, still-unoperationalized gap survives.

Therefore this goal does not need a new founder ontology decision. It needs to
respect the one already frozen. Reopening that choice would convert a reader
diagnosis into product redesign without new evidence.

## What would falsify this diagnosis

The primary diagnosis would be false or materially weakened if a future,
separately authorized experiment showed any of the following while keeping all
other factors fixed:

- removing only the prior interpretation makes Case 04 complete with zero,
  while the same broad semantic task otherwise remains unchanged; that would
  promote context-authority anchoring to the primary cause;
- separating only the two surfaces makes Case 04 complete with zero, while the
  same semantic definitions and prior remain; that would promote task-shape
  pressure;
- preserving task, prior, and pairing but changing only the operator or
  reasoning/context envelope fixes restraint; that would promote model/context
  limitation;
- a source-first founder review decides that undefined redesign milestones are
  themselves a legitimate residual in Case 04; that would invalidate the
  expected-zero ontology rather than the reader;
- a semantic-task-only change still produces the same threshold, fallback, and
  fixed-review records while Case 01 remains comparable; that would show the
  single repair type was insufficient and require stop or a separately scoped
  causal split.

No such counterfactual is run or prepared here.
