# Reasoning-process ground-up evidence audit

Status: v1 and v2 model-backed paths failed; v2.1 semantic contract passes locally; new-case evidence required  
Date: 2026-07-12

Update: the provider-free work authorized by this audit was completed. V1
preserved reviewed fixtures locally, but its reserved live probe showed that
the combined role-trajectory call remained a semantic bottleneck. Role-first
v2 now passes provider-free and adversarial gates. The historical sections
below preserve the questions as they stood before execution; the outcome is
recorded at the end.

## What is proven locally

- The original conversation and exact evidence aliases remain authoritative.
- The role-explicit representation is useful: starting, current,
  qualification, and trajectory failures become inspectable.
- Belief, action/proposal, intended outcome, acceptance/willingness, and
  reported-position objects should not be collapsed into one force label.
- The v4.2 provider schema is serviceable through several non-Google operators;
  Google's failure is not a universal schema limit.
- Wire acceptance, deterministic admission, and source fidelity are distinct.
- Four July 2026 model/operator pairs failed the combined contract's source
  review; a stronger model did not reliably repair it.
- One prompt-only role-coverage clarification did not repair the source-level
  defect, so another combined prompt variant is not justified.
- DeepSeek V4 Flash/Alibaba is the closest low-cost development reference, not
  a production selection.
- The reserved agency case has not been exposed.

## What is strongly supported but not proven

- The combined position-plus-stance job is overloaded. Cross-model failures,
  current research, and the v1 reserved probe now support this directly. V1's
  smaller role-trajectory call still omitted protected qualification evidence,
  so merely moving stance fields to later calls was insufficient.
- Smaller semantic jobs are more likely to preserve source roles. This is the
  next falsifiable hypothesis, not an established capability.
- Provider-free decomposition can preserve the product boundary if code joins
  only exact role/evidence identities and never infers semantic compatibility.

## What remains unknown

- Whether temporal-role extraction itself is reliable on ambiguous multi-turn
  language.
- Whether stance-object errors persist after roles are supplied explicitly.
- Whether splitting increases omission through transfer between calls.
- Whether fan-out and fan-in remain bounded enough for the product.
- Whether a later free-form-first/delayed-packaging design is necessary.
- Which model/operator pair is best after the task is decomposed.
- Whether any of this improves downstream reconsideration or receipt value.

## Readiness decision

We do not understand the system “completely”; that would be an overclaim. We do
understand the current failure well enough to choose the next experiment from
first principles and external evidence.

The originally authorized work was provider-free only:

1. define a small role-trajectory response contract;
2. define separate per-role stance-object contracts;
3. define an exact-ID join that preserves missingness and disagreement;
4. replay reviewed fixtures;
5. measure schema size, maximum calls, candidates, and fan-in;
6. stop before provider calls and review whether the design actually reduced
   semantic load rather than moving it.

## Outcome after the authorized work

- V1 passed local representational and adversarial gates on seven eligible
  position fixtures.
- One reserved agency-acquisition call passed wire and route checks but failed
  deterministic and source review. It contradicted its own starting evidence,
  omitted e057 qualification evidence, and stopped before stance calls.
- The frozen v1 join also exposed a false-complete empty-join defect.
- Role-first v2 now makes starting, current, and qualification independent jobs
  and relates admitted records in a fourth exact-ID job.
- V2 replays all eight reviewed positions with 24 role records, eight
  relationships, eight complete joins, zero quarantine, a four-call ceiling,
  and maximum 4,017-byte prompt.
- All existing position cases are now exposed. Another provider call requires
  a newly frozen ambiguous multi-turn development case and is not currently
  authorized.
- That new journalism case was subsequently frozen and tested. DeepSeek served
  four calls but fragmented roles and omitted protected e056. GLM 5.2 ran the
  unchanged stronger-model control, returned empty starting and qualification
  roles, and repeated current-role fragmentation.
- Model capacity is not a sufficient explanation. Endpoint, coherent-record,
  component, coverage, and speaker-ownership ambiguity are now the leading
  causes.
- Prompt/packet-only v2.1 clarifies those meanings while keeping response
  schemas and validators unchanged. Nine reviewed cases, 27 role records, nine
  relationships, and nine joins pass provider-free; no v2.1 call is authorized
  without another new pre-frozen case.
