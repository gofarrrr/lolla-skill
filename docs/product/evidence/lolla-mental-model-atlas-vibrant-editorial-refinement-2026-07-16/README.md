# Mental Model Atlas vibrant-editorial browser evidence

Status: local founder-review evidence; unpublished

Date: 2026-07-16

This packet records the final route-scoped visual refinement of
`/models/abstraction`. It is additive to the earlier card-first, guided-reader,
and relational-aesthetic packets. Those earlier screenshots remain historical
evidence; this directory is the current visual review surface.

The design uses cool-gray instrument paper, dark-blue structural ink, and three
functional accent roles: lime for source/current selection, teal for action and
practice, and purple for the derived relationship layer. Relationship category
is deliberately not color-coded: ally is solid, tension dotted, and antagonist
dashed with a cross, always with written labels and authored direction.

The screenshots are:

1. `01-desktop-hero.png` — model identity, source cues, exact relation synopsis,
   and the four-stop page signal path;
2. `02-desktop-guided-step-3.png` — sticky, single-chapter source reading;
3. `03-desktop-practice-layer.png` — the teal practical-use layer;
4. `04-desktop-relations-ally.png` — ally index and selected exact relation;
5. `05-desktop-relations-tension.png` — dotted tension grammar;
6. `06-desktop-relations-antagonist.png` — dashed-cross antagonist grammar;
7. `07-grayscale-relation-grammar.png` — non-color relationship distinction;
8. `08-mobile-hero.png` — 390-pixel identity and source orientation;
9. `09-mobile-guided-step-3.png` — mobile sticky chapter re-orientation;
10. `10-mobile-relations-tension.png` — mobile relation selection;
11. `11-narrow-mobile-320.png` — narrow containment and one-line identity;
12. `12-mobile-failure-recovery.png` — branded, actionable source failure.

Browser checks covered 1,440×1,000, 390×844, and 320×780. The 390- and
320-pixel documents had no horizontal overflow. Every visible non-inline
interactive target at 320 pixels measured at least 44 by 44 pixels; the deep
relationship and skip links were also normalized to 44 pixels high. The mobile
motion control retains a visible Pause/Resume label and state-changing icon.
The exact relation set remained 12 records, the relationship tabs passed
arrow-key switching, the source failure recovered after its route was restored,
and reduced motion used the paused fallback.

This packet does not approve the other 221 model pages, the global graph
renderer, source publication rights, native screen-reader behavior, Teacher
journeys, product usefulness, runtime integration, or public deployment.
