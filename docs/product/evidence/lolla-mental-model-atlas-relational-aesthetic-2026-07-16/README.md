# Mental Model Atlas relational-aesthetic browser evidence

Status: local founder-review evidence; unpublished

Date: 2026-07-16

This packet records the route-scoped visual and interaction follow-up for the
local `/models/abstraction` tracer. The design combines a light editorial field,
thin outlined boxes, and explicit relationship paths with restrained segmented
color. Color repeats written relationship meaning; it does not encode rank,
confidence, relevance, or truth.

Real-browser checks covered 1440×900, 390×844, and 320×780. The final desktop
page measured 6,222 pixels high, with a 688-pixel hero and a 1,554-pixel
relationship section. The 390-pixel page had no document-level horizontal
overflow. At 320 pixels the document width also remained exactly the viewport
width; the orientation and chapter rails retain intentionally contained
horizontal scrolling.

After direct chapter selection, the desktop reader stage and its orientation
landed at 205 pixels from the viewport top. On mobile, the 125-pixel site header
ended before the chapter rail began at 128 pixels; the rail ended at 270 pixels
and the new chapter orientation began at 296 pixels.
The relationship explorer retained all 12 exact records in the DOM while showing
one semantic group and one selected record detail at a time.

This packet does not approve publication, teaching usefulness, the other 221
model pages, or a system-wide graph-renderer redesign.
