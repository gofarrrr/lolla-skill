  -> rebuild its exact incident neighborhood
  -> page high-fan-in relations without hidden truncation

## Exact next product decision

The next Atlas goal is a provider-free canonical human acceptance review, not
another architecture or styling pass. A cold reviewer should exercise the
ordinary flow on desktop and mobile, with mouse, keyboard, reduced motion, and
a native screen reader, and determine whether:

1. the 16-model orientation honestly leads into the complete canonical graph;
2. changing centers and paging relations remains understandable;
3. direction and relation kind are perceivable without relying on animation or
   color;
4. complete-card and summary-only pages cannot be confused;
5. the Abstraction reading journey helps a human understand the model and its
   exact relationships; and
6. remaining friction is specific enough to justify a bounded repair.

The review must preserve failures and disagreements rather than averaging them
into a score. Its terminal decision should be one of: authorize a narrowly
defined repair, authorize a small representative complete-page expansion after
rights review, or keep the Atlas at the current experimental baseline. It must
not authorize deployment, mass page generation, provider use, Teacher revival,
or product-usefulness claims by implication.
