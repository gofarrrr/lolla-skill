Boundary: Optionality has become a permanent excuse not to commit even after evidence has clarified which path has the best economics or strategic fit.

## Relation Story

Cognitive Load Theory and Optionality form a structured tension pair. Cognitive Load Theory conflicts with Optionality when too many parallel branches exceed practical decision bandwidth. Use the pair as a calibration exercise: do not maximize either lens. Cognitive Load Theory protects against overload, while Optionality protects against premature closure. The lesson is to choose the smallest set of options that still preserves learning.
