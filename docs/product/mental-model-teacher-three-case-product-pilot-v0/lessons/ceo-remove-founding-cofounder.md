## Model Stack

- [Name The Feeling Without Letting It Rule](../../../../reviews/codex-assisted/mental-model-teacher-knowledge-mesh-v2/ceo-remove-founding-cofounder/okf/mental_model_teacher/models/emotional-intelligence.md) - `primary_teaching_lens`: When the technically correct answer may fail because emotions, trust, morale, or perceived fairness shape adoption, require the answer to identify the emotional signal, validate it, and preserve standards, evidence, and accountability.
Boundary: Empathy is being treated as a substitute for standards, evidence, or hard conversations.
- [Make The Failure Visible First](../../../../reviews/codex-assisted/mental-model-teacher-knowledge-mesh-v2/ceo-remove-founding-cofounder/okf/mental_model_teacher/models/premortem.md) - `primary_teaching_lens`: Before commitment, treat the plan as already failed, work backward through plausible causes and domino effects, then convert controllable failure paths into mitigations, owners, reversal thresholds, or decision changes.
Boundary: The exercise is producing an obvious list of worries without owners, mitigations, or decision changes tied to what was surfaced.
- [Turn The Plan Upside Down](../../../../reviews/codex-assisted/mental-model-teacher-knowledge-mesh-v2/ceo-remove-founding-cofounder/okf/mental_model_teacher/models/inversion.md) - `boundary_lens`: Reverse the desired outcome into a concrete non-goal, identify the actions that would create that failure, and remove or avoid those actions before committing resources.
Boundary: Inversion is being used as a substitute for execution - identifying what to avoid without subsequently taking positive action leads to paralysis, not progress.

## Relation Story

Inversion and Premortem form an ally pair. Premortem is a direct procedural application of Inversion - it operationalizes backward-from-failure reasoning into a repeatable team exercise that overcomes groupthink. Use the pair as a sequence: Inversion supplies the direction of the reasoning move, and Premortem turns that move into a repeatable procedure. The lesson is not to choose between them, but to let one make the other usable.

## Worked Example

- Weak first read: The cofounder situation is emotionally hard, so either empathy or removal should dominate the answer.
- Better question: What feeling is present, what fair rule would still apply if that feeling changed, and what failure would prove the transition unsafe?
- Stronger answer: Treat loyalty, grief, or trust as data; apply a role-clarity and accountability rule; then premortem customer, team, and authority failure conditions.
